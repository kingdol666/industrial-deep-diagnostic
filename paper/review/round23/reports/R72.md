## 7. Assessment

The paper is that rare artifact where every number, path, timestamp, and code-level claim I
probed (12 spot checks spanning metrics, chronology, artifact index, engine registry, rubric
gate, package inventory, baselines, RAG channel, repair counts) reproduces exactly from the
released tree, and where the negatives (bare-LLM superiority, single-execution evidence,
author-designed instruments, inline execution form, control coverage gap, unstable period
estimate, seeded-draw gap) are stated more prominently than the positives. The one structural
soft spot — the graded runs executed inline rather than through the sub-agent dispatch machinery
that §3 describes — is disclosed repeatedly and precisely (per-run log markers verified), and the
artifact/gate machinery it substitutes is itself real and verified. For AEI's audience (system
clarity, module contracts, reproducibility, honest negatives), this is close to the mark.

Remaining issues are presentational, not correctness: length/repetition, abstract density, and
one discoverability improvement for the execution-form disclosure.

VERDICT: MINOR_REVISION.

### MUST-FIX (blocking, small)
1. §1/Contribution 4: state once, near the benchmark contribution itself, that the graded runs
   executed inline under their skill protocols (currently the disclosure first lands in §5; a
   reader of the contributions list should not discover the execution form 20 pages later).
2. Whole paper: condense the repeated disclosures (the 5/9 + 4/6 pre-revision figures appear in
   ~9 places; the inline-execution disclosure in ~4); target a materially shorter manuscript —
   75 pp in 12pt preprint format is beyond what AEI readers expect, and the repetition is the
   single largest controllable contributor.
3. Fig 3(a): add an in-panel legend for the dark-blue / hatched-sky / amber classes (the printed
   fractions "0/2", "5/6", "1/1" are unambiguous only with the caption in hand; "0/2" for SKAB
   will be misread by skimmers as a missing bar).

### SHOULD-FIX (non-blocking)
1. Abstract: the final sentence's scope ("Internal consistency is machine-verified...") is
   correct but easy to over-read in isolation; consider "Internal consistency is machine-verified
   against the released execution proofs (not independently re-executed)" if a few words can be
   freed by trimming earlier sentences.
2. §7.2/Table 8: state explicitly that the "decisive signature" column is read from the single
   canonical execution named in each grading record (the IDV14 caveat does this for the stability
   pair; the same one-sentence bound would serve Table 8).
3. §5 C1: the parenthetical explaining that the journal `calibrated` field records verdict-type
   compliance is load-bearing for interpreting Table 4 (skab_valve1_1 stays `calibrated:false`
   throughout); consider promoting it out of parenthesis.
4. Table 9: add a footnote that the FE-official arm's miss (IDV4) is under FE's own T2-contribution
   features on this deployment, i.e. not FE's published GPT-4o/o1 numbers (the text says this;
   the table caption could carry it).
5. §9 "Cost": the 56-minute lower bound comes from tep_d14 (55.6 min); consider "55–220" or "≈56"
   to survive rounding scrutiny.
6. Consider a one-line pointer from Table 5 to the actual released tree (the run-directory naming
   convention `<ts>_bench_<case>` appears only in Table A2's caption).
