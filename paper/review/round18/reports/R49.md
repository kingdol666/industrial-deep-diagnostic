## 6. Cost disclosure — range MATCH, median MISMATCH

Paper states three times "56–220 minutes ... median ≈131 minutes **across the twelve canonical
runs**, recorded in the event logs" (tex:437 §6.4; tex:551 §8.1; tex:651 §9 Cost). I computed
first→last timestamp spans of `.pipeline_events.jsonl` for all twelve canonical run dirs named in
`results/benchmark/gradings/*.json`:

```
tep_d14  55.6 | tep_d00  65.9 | skab_valve 69.4 | skab_cavit 74.7 | skab_norm 81.1 | tep_d01 86.6
tep_d07 131.5 | tep_d11 136.5 | tep_d04 141.1 | tep_d03 146.4 | batch001 217.4 | batch093 220.1  (minutes)
```

- Range: min 55.6 → "56", max 220.1 → "220" — **MATCH**.
- Median across the twelve runs: **109.05 min** — not ≈131. The value 131.5 is the median across
  the **nine fault scenarios only** (5th of 9 sorted). The paper attaches "median ≈131" to the
  twelve-run denominator twice (tex:437, tex:651) and unqualified once (tex:551).
- **MISMATCH (must fix)**: either report median ≈109 across the twelve canonical runs, or median
  ≈131 across the nine faults — and say which denominator. Recommend also releasing the per-run
  span table so the statistic is checkable (the paper already cites the logs as the source).

numbers, CIs, caps, chronology, sensitivity, stability, determinism, baselines, case study —
reproduced exactly. These are one-line fixes; no re-analysis is required. MINOR_REVISION.
