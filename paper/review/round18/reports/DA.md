# Devil's Advocate Review — Round 18

**Manuscript:** `paper/main.tex` — "Trustworthy agentic diagnosis for industrial processes: evidence-graded competing-hypothesis reasoning evaluated against per-fault literature baselines"
**Reviewer role:** Devil's Advocate (strongest publishable case against acceptance)
**Verification basis:** manuscript plus read-only inspection of `results/benchmark/*` (metrics.json, baselines.json, gradings/*, consistency_audit.json, retest_selection.json, suite_determinism.json), `scripts/benchmark/cases/benchmark_cases.json`, `scripts/benchmark/run-tier.mjs`, and `workspace/diagnostic-runs/2026091716*_bench_*`, `202609172247007_bench_*`.

## 1. Strongest Counter-Argument (steel-man, then test the paper's defenses)

Strip away the machinery and the paper reports: a bare call to the same model family, given the same blind digest, answers 9/9 documented faults correctly under the paper's own strict keywords (`baselines.json`), in minutes; the 9-stage pipeline answers 4/9 (44.4%, Wilson CI [18.9, 73.3] — an interval that overlaps a coin flip at p=0.5), costs roughly two hours of agent time per scenario, and returns non-answers ("capped COMPETING_SET") on five of nine faults. Every instrument that flatters the pipeline is author-designed: the scenario set, the grading keywords, the expected-verdict sets, the rubric, the ceiling arithmetic. The headline "top-k 9/9" is close to unfalsifiable by construction: the grader (run-tier.mjs:285–291) substring-searches the truth keywords across the primary finding and every surviving hypothesis's names, logic chains, evidence lists, and ontology proofs — thousands of words per scenario — using tokens as generic as "header", "stream 2", "random", and "deviation". Stability rests on exactly one consistency pair. One model family, one deployment, one harness, single executions. A practitioner reading the five capped verdicts sees a system that declines to diagnose half the time, and the paper's own compliance metric cannot penalize that (8/9 scenarios' allowed sets admit COMPETING_SET), so a maximally evasive system would score 8/9 on verdict-type compliance while resolving nothing.

**Do the paper's defenses survive?** Partially — and this must be said honestly. The paper does *not* hide the adverse facts: the abstract itself states the bare call scores 9/9 "more than the pipeline"; the ablation table leads with it; the five caps are counted, plotted, and one is scored as a compliance miss; the CI is printed; the rubric is admitted to be a conformance instrument with "limited discriminative power"; internal-only verification is conceded (C5, and "events are pipeline-internal records, not externally signed attestations"). This is unusually candid writing, and the three-state framing (DETERMINED/COMPETING_SET/NEEDS_DATA) is a real, coherently implemented design. **But** the defenses are disclosures, not demonstrations, and in the places where the paper converts its artifacts into headline claims, the released artifacts contradict the prose (Section 3, Issue CR-1). The audit plane, execution proofs, and honesty framing survive as *engineering*; the *evaluative* claims built on top of them — top-k 9/9 as "the stronger statement the sample size can support" (§8.5/Discussion), the blind-set success narrative, "resolves 4/9 faults to a single root cause" (Abstract) — do not survive at the strength asserted.

## 2. Cherry-Picking / Confirmation-Bias Scan

**What is honestly presented.** The 4/9, the CI, the five caps, the two 0.90-gate violations (0.91, 0.93), the rubric deductions (85/85/65), and the 9/9 bare-LLM result are all stated in the abstract, Table 2 (tab:overall), and Section 7.1/8.3. The rubric deductions are explained, not glossed: SKAB valve −15 (verdict-type), IDV1 −15 (0.91 vs 0.90), IndPenSim control −35 (−15 calibration, −20 grounding). The claim "twelve of thirteen runs closed with zero diagnosis-layer repairs" is verified (one `repair_spawn` event in the d03 log).

**What is narratively advantaged.**

- **The blind-set success is overstated relative to its own artifact.** The paper claims twice — §6.2 ("with the cavitation-type family top-ranked") and the Discussion contamination paragraph ("still yielded a correctly top-ranked (though capped) cavitation-type mechanism") — that the withheld-label SKAB scenario ranked cavitation first. The released `diagnosis.json` (run 202609171630579) shows H1 = "阀门深节流/自激振荡类…" (valve deep-throttling, conf 64, listed first) and H2 = "吸入侧汽蚀/掺气失稳类…" (cavitation, conf 64, listed second). The true mechanism is tied, not top; the head of the list is the competing valve-side hypothesis. The aggregate report's own text says only *two* of five CS cases have the truth first-ranked — cavitation is not one of them.
- **The SKAB valve top-k hit rides on the wrong hypothesis.** In run 202609171630389 the top-ranked survivor is *downstream-side* throttling (65); the truth is the *suction-side* valve restriction, which is H2 (60). The keyword hit ("节流") is already present in the wrong-side H1, so the instrument would have returned topk=true even had the true-side hypothesis been absent. "True mechanism ranked in all 5" is technically true here; the instrument that certifies it could not have caught its violation.
- **Top-k 9/9 is promoted before its weakness is disclosed.** The abstract and Fig. 5 headline top-k = 9/9 with CI [70.1, 100]; the disclosure that this searches all survivor text with generic tokens comes pages later (§7.2, and only partially). The Discussion calls top-k "the stronger statement the current sample size can support" — it is in fact the *loosest* instrument in the paper.
- Minor: Fig. 1's caption "no scenario's survivors exclude the true mechanism" is artifact-true but instrument-blind for the reason above.

## 3. Logic-Chain Validation (two load-bearing chains attacked)

### 3.1 TEP d07 — "supply-capacity drop" from XMV_4 gain loss (DETERMINED, 0.78; paper §8.1, §4.1)

The chain: XMV_4 opens +25.13% (z=12.35, sole envelope-breaking channel) at constant flow (−0.03%) ⇒ by Q = Cv·f(x)·√(ΔP/ρ), ΔP falls to ratio 0.638 ⇒ Stream-4 header supply pressure stepped down. Attack:

1. **The algebra assumes a healthy, time-invariant valve.** Cv, f(x), and ρ are held constant across eras. A degrading valve (seat wear, developing stiction, positioner fault) also forces the controller open-loop-integral to open the valve at constant flow — the identical signature. The pipeline does *not* eliminate the valve-internal alternative by any cited discriminating signature; contrast d14, where quantised stepping is (reasonably) used as the actuator fingerprint. In d07 the valve-internal fork is simply not run.
2. **"DETERMINED" over an unmeasured node, conceded in the artifact itself.** The diagnosis's own root_physical_cause field declares the header pressure "未测量推断节点" and the equipment-level cause "超出遥测数据可判别范围", and the confidence is capped at 0.78 under reason-code ALL_CRITERIA_VERIFIED *because the root variable is unobservable*. A verdict that is simultaneously "determined to a single root cause" (Abstract) and "the physical cause is beyond what the data can identify" (artifact) is a category the top-1 metric flattens. What is determined is a mechanism class credited by family-level keywords ("header", "c feed", "stream 4" — tokens that also substring-match IDV1's "A/C feed ratio" answer, so the grader could not have distinguished an IDV1 answer).
3. **Recall is visible inside the blind artifact.** H1's name is "…（C-header压力损失，IDV(7)类机制）" — the documented catalogue label, inside the artifact that earns the keyword hit. The same is true for d01 ("IDV1"). The statistics are real (the numbers reproduce), but the "statistics-first" framing coexists with textbook label-naming in two of six TEP diagnoses, and the ontology itself injects published TEP semantics.

**Verdict:** the chain is physically literate and the strongest of the four DETs, but "resolves to a single root cause" is an overstatement of what is a one-sided fork with the healthy-valve assumption unstated and the root node admittedly unmeasured.

### 3.2 TEP d14 — "valve stiction" from quantised stepping (DETERMINED, 0.80; paper §7.2, Table 3)

The chain: variance explosions ×189/116/222 with frozen means, |ΔXMV_10| p95 ×16.2 (633/800 intervals >5× baseline p95), 21-min limit cycle, lag structure 1→5, loop-locality ⇒ XMV_10 stiction. Attack:

1. **The discriminating channel for stiction is absent.** Classical stiction identification (Choudhury-style) requires controller output (OP) versus valve position (MV). XMV_10 is a single signal; OP-vs-position separation — the one measurement that separates *valve* stiction from *controller* mis-tuning, deadband, or an externally cycling loop — does not exist in the recording. The elimination list (sensor artifact, global feed/regime switch, recycle-gas throttling, steam side, plant-wide cooling co-source) contains **no actuator-family-internal alternative**: deadband and aggressive retuning are never stated, tested, or excluded. "Stick-slip-like MV stepping" is what the data establish; "stiction" (a specific packing/friction failure mode) is the textbook label that the keyword set ("sticking", "粘滞") was built to credit.
2. **Asymmetric cap discipline.** SKAB valve gets a cap because valve-position/command channels are absent; d14 gets DETERMINED 0.80 although the valve-internal state is equally unobservable and its sub-mechanism equally unidentified. The DET/CS boundary is drawn at the granularity of the author-chosen hypothesis vocabulary, not at a data-identifiability threshold — and the benchmark's expected-verdict sets accept either.
3. **Same-model contamination ceiling.** The bare LLM answers "IDV(14): Reactor cooling water valve sticking" from the catalogue. The pipeline reaches the same word via statistics — that is the paper's genuine differentiator and the lag/VR/locality work is real — but nothing in the corpus can show the *conclusion* was statistics-caused rather than recall-primed and statistics-confirmed. The form-discrimination argument (IDV4/11/14) is the right mitigation design; it is evidence of *capability*, not proof of *causation* in any single verdict.

**Verdict:** defensible as "loop-internal oscillation, actuator the probable site"; over-specified as a DETERMINED mechanism identity at 0.80.

## 4. Overgeneralization Scan

- **"Auditable engineering procedure" (Conclusion, §1) vs internal-only verification.** The audit plane is self-authored: event logs are "pipeline-internal records, not externally signed attestations" (§3.4, admitted); the rubric measures conformance to the paper's own protocol (§7.2, admitted); independent expert anchoring is "outstanding" (Discussion, admitted). What remains overgeneralized is the title-level "Trustworthy" and the conclusion's "turns LLM-based root-cause analysis into an auditable engineering procedure" — auditability *of process conformance* is demonstrated; auditability *of truth* is not, and no external checker exists anywhere in the loop.
- **"No verdict rests on external evidence" (§3.2) vs RAG fallback provenance.** Verified: 11 of 13 runs carry `rag_deep_understanding.json` with `rag_available: false` and web research skipped; no L6-tagged item appears in surviving chains. **But the sentence is false as written for 2 of 13 runs**: tep_d04 (the case study) and tep_d07 (a Top-1 showcase) contain no `rag_deep_understanding.json` anywhere in their run directories (directory listings inspected) — so "each run's rag_deep_understanding.json carries the probe-failure record" and "all thirteen recorded executions record a failed health probe" are contradicted by the released corpus. Deeper problem: the L1–L7 scheme grades *cited* evidence only; the ontology fallback is "parameter_to_physics.json + 过程知识推理" — i.e., the model's internalized (ungraded, untaggable) textbook knowledge, including the TEP catalogue. Technically tag-true, epistemically misleading: every TEP verdict rests partly on external knowledge the grading system cannot see.
- **"Resolves 4/9 faults to a single root cause" (Abstract, Conclusion)** — as shown in §3, two of the four are mechanism-class resolutions whose own artifacts declare the physical cause beyond the data. "Single root cause" should read "single surviving mechanism-class hypothesis".
- **GxP/ALCOA+/GAMP 5 framing (Discussion)** is appropriately hedged ("not a computerised-system validation claim") — no overgeneralization here; noted fairly.

## 5. "So What?" Test

The paper concedes the central number: bare LLM 9/9 (verified in `baselines.json`; the TEP answers are catalogue recitations — "IDV(7): C header pressure reduced…" — i.e., recall, at minutes of cost) versus the pipeline's 4/9 at ~2 h/scenario. The claimed margins are auditability, calibrated uncertainty, and gated process. Do these margins demonstrably change any decision, relative to the bare call?

- **Auditability:** demonstrated as artifact conformance (rubric, gates, proofs — all verified present), but no result shows the audit plane catching an error that mattered: the paper states repairs "changed no verdict text", the two 0.90-gate violations were caught by the author's own rubric (a real catch, trivially minor), and the fabricated-artifact shortcut episode is self-detected, self-disclosed history.
- **Calibrated uncertainty:** zero overconfidence (4/4 DET correct) is a genuine datum — but n=4, single executions, and the gate was breached twice by 0.01/0.03. The psychometric calibration study is admitted to be future work.
- **Capped verdicts as "work orders for targeted instrumentation":** plausible and well-argued, but never tested — no evidence that any named discriminating measurement (cooling-water inlet thermocouple, steam-header temperature) would in fact collapse its fork, no expert comparison, and the deployment anecdote (film line, NEEDS_DATA) is n=1 and unbenchmarkable.
- **Decision-value asymmetry:** for a practitioner, "IDV7: C header pressure loss" in 3 minutes with a top-3 list is actionable; "COMPETING_SET 0.60, install a thermocouple" in 2 hours may be *more honest* but the paper offers no evidence it leads to better outcomes — the very comparison (expert-with-digest vs pipeline, or bare-LLM-plus-engineer-review vs pipeline) that would demonstrate this is named as outstanding.

The honest answer to "so what" is that the paper *reframes* a negative accuracy result as a process contribution and largely discloses that this is a bet, not a finding. That is publishable as an honest engineering paper only if the evaluative instruments are beyond reproach — which Issue CR-1 shows they currently are not.

## 6. Categorized Issues

### CRITICAL

- **CR-1. Prose contradicts the released artifacts in the exact places that carry the headline claims.** (i) §6.2 ("cavitation-type family top-ranked") and Discussion ("correctly top-ranked … cavitation-type mechanism") vs `202609171630579_bench_skab_cavitation_13/04_diagnostics/diagnosis.json`: cavitation is H2 of 2, tied at 64, listed below the valve-side hypothesis. (ii) §3.2 "all thirteen recorded executions record a failed health probe … each run's rag_deep_understanding.json carries the probe-failure record" vs runs 202609171639066 (tep_d04) and 202609171639214 (tep_d07): no such file exists anywhere in either run directory. A paper whose sole reason to exist is "machine-checked artifact–text consistency" cannot ship sentences its own artifacts falsify. This is fixable by rewording and re-scoping, but until fixed it blocks publication, because it invalidates the blind-set contamination defense and the RAG-degradation completeness claim — both of which are load-bearing for the "trustworthy" thesis.

### MAJOR

- **MA-1. The top-k instrument cannot support "9/9, CI [70.1,100]" at headline strength.** Keywords are substring-matched over primary finding + all survivor chains/evidence/ontology proofs (run-tier.mjs:285–291) with family-generic tokens ("header", "c feed" — which substring-matches an IDV1 answer — "stream 2", "random", "deviation", "ph "). The SKAB-valve case proves the false-positive mode: the hit token lives in the *wrong-side* top-ranked hypothesis. "Approximates candidate-list membership" (§7.2) is the right description; abstract/conclusion/Fig. 5 elevation of 9/9 is not.
- **MA-2. Top-1 verdict semantics are inflated.** d07 (0.78) and d14 (0.80) are DETERMINED over unmeasured/inaccessible root nodes, with the healthy-valve assumption (d07) and the stiction-vs-deadband/tuning fork (d14) unresolved and partly unacknowledged; d07's artifact concedes the root variable is unobservable. Abstract's "resolves 4/9 faults to a single root cause" should be mechanism-class-level resolution; the DET/CS boundary is vocabulary-relative, and the compliance metric is one-directional (admitted at §7.3): 8/9 allowed sets admit CS, so systematic under-resolution is invisible to every metric except Top-1 — which is exactly the metric the pipeline loses on.
- **MA-3. Single-execution, single-model, single-harness evidence base for *process* guarantees.** Disclosed, and the one consistency pair (d11, Δconf 0.02) is real and verified — but the pair's mechanism texts diverge to token-overlap 0.1417, far under the audit's own 0.35 relevance threshold, passing only via the lenient tag+class rule; R2's top-ranked mechanism ("分支级结论…") is noticeably vaguer than R1's. One lenient pair cannot ground "verdict stability" as a property; the paper's own "one pair is the beginning, not the end" is the correct sentence — the abstract's "Consistency is tested…" framing is stronger than the content.
- **MA-4. Bare-LLM 9/9 arm is a single replay scored by the same author's keywords against catalogue-recitation answers.** The comparison register is fair (same digest, strict scoring) but the 9/9 is one stochastic sample of the model reading a public fault table; the paper knows this ("plausibly by literature recall") yet Table 6's row invites the 9/9-vs-4/9 cost reading without repeating either arm.

### MINOR

- **MI-1.** §3.2 claims ontology fast-reuse "is exercised by the production harness" but the benchmark pinned full rebuilds and "none was consumed" — the amortization economics in the Discussion (§7, deployment) therefore rest on an unexercised-in-benchmark path.
- **MI-2.** Table 2 note "its interval is therefore omitted" for determined-precision (4/4) is correct reasoning, but the row still invites the "precision 100%" glance; consider dropping it from the headline table.
- **MI-3.** FE General-Reasoning "8/11" is transcribed despite the paper itself noting FE's internal inconsistency on that number — properly footnoted, but a transcription of a suspect figure nonetheless.
- **MI-4.** Judge scores (90–98) appear in Table 2/3 next to rubric scores; the caption disclaims benchmark weight, but proximity invites conflation.
- **MI-5.** The "eight of thirteen runs rejected or bounced by gates" (§3.4) is a soft count not independently recomputed in this review (plausible from released review artifacts; unverified).

## 7. The Two Issues the Authors MUST Answer — and whether the existing defenses already answer them

**Q1. Do the manuscript's claims match its own released artifacts where those claims are load-bearing (blind-set ranking, RAG-probe completeness, top-k semantics), and will the authors re-scope or correct them?**
*Defense status: does NOT hold as written.* The paper's disclosures (keyword looseness, rubric scope, one generic token) are real but do not cover sentences that contradict the artifacts: cavitation is not "top-ranked" in `diagnosis.json`, two runs have no RAG probe record at all, and top-k as implemented searches every survivor's full text with family-generic tokens. Correction is cheap (reword, add a first-ranked survivor statistic, re-scope top-k to "family-level candidate membership" everywhere including abstract and Fig. 5); refusal to correct is disqualifying, because the paper's entire contribution is that its text is machine-checkable against its artifacts.

**Q2. Given bare-LLM 9/9 at ~100× lower cost, what decision-relevant capability does the pipeline demonstrate *on this corpus* — beyond the honest three-state framing — and can the authors name one concrete decision the audit/calibration machinery changed or would change?**
*Defense status: PARTIALLY holds.* The honesty defenses are genuine and verified (caps enforced; the one expected-DETERMINED miss counted as a miss; zero overconfidence on n=4; adverse ablation printed in the abstract — rare and commendable). But partial candor is not a demonstration: no expert comparison, no evidence the named discriminating measurements resolve their forks, no case where the audit plane caught a truth-threatening defect (all repairs changed no verdict text), and the compliance metric is structurally blind to under-resolution. The paper's own "on documented faults the pipeline's value is process quality, not accuracy" is a hypothesis this benchmark cannot confirm — the authors must either run the planned expert/blind-digest comparison, or explicitly re-price the contribution as a framework + protocol paper whose empirical section is a negative result with instrumentation, and let the venue judge that framing.

---

**Summary verdict (Devil's Advocate):** Not acceptable in current form. The engineering honesty is real and unusual; the artifact corpus is genuinely released and mostly supports the numbers (4/9, CI, rubric deductions, determinism 45/45, seeded retest all verified). But the paper overstating its loosest instrument (top-k), inflating two mechanism-class verdicts into "single root cause" resolutions, and — decisively — carrying prose its own artifacts contradict, breaches the exact standard it asks to be evaluated by. Fix CR-1 and MA-1/MA-2 by correction and re-scoping, run or explicitly descope Q2, and this could become a respectable honest-negative-result + framework paper. Until then, the strongest sentence a reviewer can write is the paper's own, turned back on it: *no result exists without an execution proof* — and two of the proofs contradict the text.
