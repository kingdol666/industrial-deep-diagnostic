# Review 1 — Methodology & Reproducibility (Advanced Engineering Informatics, round 18)

**Manuscript:** `paper/main.tex` — "Trustworthy agentic diagnosis for industrial processes: evidence-graded competing-hypothesis reasoning evaluated against per-fault literature baselines"

**Review basis:** I verified the manuscript against the released repository artifacts (results/benchmark/*, workspace/diagnostic-runs/20260917*, scripts/benchmark/*, git history). Every number below was recomputed or read directly from artifacts; I did not trust the paper's own transcription of them.

---

## 1. Summary of the submission

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage agentic diagnosis pipeline (ontology → deterministic statistics → competing-hypothesis reasoning → judge/physical-audit gates → report → HTML), with a three-state verdict protocol (DETERMINED / COMPETING_SET / NEEDS_DATA), evidence levels L1–L7, confidence ceilings, and a machine-checked evaluation contract (truth isolation, data fingerprinting, stage-authored artifacts, gate-graded scoring, artifact-consistency gate). Evaluation: 12 scenarios (9 documented faults + 3 controls) on SKAB, TEP, IndPenSim; one seeded random-draw re-execution (TEP IDV11); model-controlled baselines (bare LLM, FE-protocol replication, FE official, classical PCA); a deterministic 45-run baseline suite with a byte-level determinism check.

## 3. General assessment

Strengths, from my angle specifically:

1. **Benchmark correctness is genuinely good.** Every headline number I recomputed reproduces exactly; per-scenario verdicts, confidences, rubric deductions, keyword hits, baseline counts, PCA rates, determinism verdict, and repair accounting all match the released artifacts. The grader is deterministic, the aggregator's Wilson implementation is correct (z=1.96, verified against my own computation), and the metric-recomputation gate (`verify-repro.mjs` output) recomputes aggregates from per-scenario gradings with zero drift.
2. **Unusual candour.** The 9/9 bare-LLM result that undercuts the pipeline's accuracy is printed in the abstract; the two 0.90-gate exceedances are plotted, deducted, and analysed; the circularity of capped-verdict ceiling compliance is admitted; the rubric's non-discriminative power is admitted; the one-directionality of verdict-type compliance (can flag over-capping, never under-capping) is admitted; the keyword instrument's family-level nature is admitted. The paper repeatedly states less than it could have.
3. **Truth isolation is real, not decorative.** Blind briefs are clean (spot-checked), user contexts carry `truth_withheld`, the sentinel is exit-gated in the pipeline driver, and the keyword/expected-verdict instrumentation demonstrably predates execution (commit 221d061, 2026-09-15, before all 2026-09-17 runs).

Weaknesses are concentrated in three areas: (i) the stability evidence is one pair selected by a self-chosen seed, yet the text claims more protection than that mechanism provides (MAJOR-1); (ii) a handful of sentences about the released corpus are falsified or muddled by the corpus itself (MINOR-2, MINOR-3, MINOR-5) — for a paper whose thesis is machine-verified internal consistency, these matter more than their metric impact suggests; (iii) the top-k instrument's rank semantics are loose (MINOR-1).

## 4. MAJOR issues

**MAJOR-1 — The random-draw claim overstates what a self-selected seed guarantees.**
- **Location:** Section 7.4 (`sec:stability`), first paragraph ("a scenario is selected by a seeded uniform random draw ... **so the re-tested scenario cannot be hand-picked after seeing results**"); abstract ("a seeded uniform random draw selected one fault scenario"); Section 9 conclusion ("a random draw, executed blind").
- **Evidence:** `retest_selection.json` shows `selected_at: 2026-09-17T22:46:46Z` — i.e., the draw was executed *after* all twelve canonical runs had finished (16:30–16:40 the same day). The seed (1003818694) is an arbitrary 32-bit value authored by the same team that knew the canonical results, and mulberry32 is trivially searchable: a determined author could scan seeds until the draw lands on a favourable case (e.g., a scenario likely to reproduce rather than IDV3, the hardest). The mechanism eliminates hand-picking the *scenario*, not hand-picking the *seed*. Since the entire stability evidence is this single pair (1/1), the integrity of the selection process is material to the paper's stability claim.
- **Fix (concrete):** Either (a) temper the claim to what is true — "the draw is replayable from a released seed; the seed itself was authored by the evaluation harness operator, so seed-searching cannot be ruled out" — or, better, (b) strengthen the mechanism: derive the seed from an external unpredictable, timestamped source (NIST randomness beacon value, a future Bitcoin block hash, or a journal-timestamped pre-registration) for the remaining eleven re-run contracts, and state the source in Section 5 (C-contracts) and Section 7.4. Given the eleven outstanding re-run contracts are already pre-registered, adopting (b) for them costs little and converts the paper's weakest inference into a strong one.

**MAJOR-2 — The evaluation's core instruments are author-designed and unanchored; the paper discloses this but the revision should do more than disclose.**
- **Location:** Sections 6.1 (C1), 6.2 (Metrics), 9 (`sec:discussion`, "Inference authorship and provenance"), Table 6 footnote.
- **Evidence:** The grading keywords, `expect_type_set`, and the rubric are all authored by the system's designer, and the top-k 9/9 headline rests on them. I confirmed the keyword sets are family-level and of heterogeneous specificity: e.g. tep_d04/d11 hit on "反应器冷却/reactor cooling/冷却水" (any cooling-water hypothesis scores), skab_valve1_1 hits on "valve/阀/受限" in a loop whose *process description given to the agents already says the loop contains a valve* — so "valve/阀" cannot discriminate any hypothesis in that scenario from any other. The paper's sentence "one of them (the IndPenSim process-deviation scenario) includes a generic token" therefore **undercounts**: at least the SKAB-valve set also contains tokens that cannot fail to match. The repository even ships `scripts/benchmark/keyword_sensitivity.mjs`, but no sensitivity result is reported in the paper.
- **Fix (concrete):** (i) Correct the "one generic token" sentence to enumerate every low-specificity token per scenario; (ii) report the output of a keyword-sensitivity analysis (leave-one-keyword-out: does top-k survive?) — the released script suggests this was anticipated; (iii) add the rank of the true mechanism within each survivor set to Table 5 (see MINOR-1); (iv) since independent expert anchoring is named as outstanding, at minimum report a small blinded adjudication (even 3–5 scenarios × 2 experts) or state plainly in the abstract that top-k is keyword-instrument-dependent.

## 5. MINOR issues

**MINOR-1 — "top-k" uses a per-scenario, variable k and never reports the true mechanism's rank.**
- **Location:** Section 6.2 (Metrics, top-k definition), Table 6 row 2, abstract, conclusion.
- **Evidence:** For skab_valve1_1 the truth-side hypothesis ("上游侧供给受限…吸入侧受阻") is the *second* of two survivors (top-ranked is downstream throttling — the wrong side); for IDV3 the feed-temperature family sits among three survivors; for IDV4 it is top-ranked. "9/9 within top-k" therefore mixes rank-1 and rank-3 evidence under one number, and k varies (2–4) per scenario. This is disclosed as "approximates candidate-list membership", but the abstract's "top-k = 9/9" reads like a fixed-k metric.
- **Fix:** Add a rank column (rank of truth-keyword hit within the ranked survivor set) to Table 5 or a appendix table; in the abstract, consider "the true mechanism is named in every scenario's surviving-hypothesis set (top-ranked in 3, ranked in the remainder)" or similar.

**MINOR-2 — Factual claim about RAG probe records is false for 2 of 13 runs.**
- **Location:** Section 4.2 (`sec:arch-layers`), "Semantic-knowledge layer": "all thirteen recorded executions ... record a failed health probe ... **each run's `rag_deep_understanding.json` carries the probe-failure record**".
- **Evidence:** `rag_deep_understanding.json` does not exist in `202609171639066_bench_tep_d04_reactor_cooling_step` or `202609171639214_bench_tep_d07_header_pressure`; in those two runs the probe-failure record lives in `01_ontology/ontology.json` (which does contain rag/probe/unavailable/8764 markers). The substantive claim (all 13 runs degraded; no L6 evidence) survives, but the sentence as written is checkably wrong.
- **Fix:** Reword to "eleven runs carry the record in `rag_deep_understanding.json`; in the two runs where that file was not emitted, the probe failure is recorded in the ontology's provenance block" — or restore/emit the two missing files and re-run the consistency gate.

**MINOR-3 — The round-1 judge score "75 with two blocking issues" (IDV3) is not locatable in the released artifacts.**
- **Location:** Section 4.4 (Audit infrastructure: "the released review artifacts record the round-1 judge fail at 75 with two blocking issues"); repeated in Sections 6.4 and 9.
- **Evidence:** The IDV3 run directory's `05_review/judge_feedback.json` holds only the round-2 record (iteration 2, score 90); no file under the run dir or `results/benchmark/` contains `overall_score: 75`. The *event* is proven (`.pipeline_events.jsonl` has `repair_spawn` round 1, source judge, followed by a judge `agent_start` at repair_round 2), and the round-2 verdict_rule references the two round-1 blocking dimensions — but the number 75 itself is unciteable.
- **Fix:** Archive round-1 review files (e.g. `05_review/judge_feedback_round1.json`, with round-2 files versioned) or soften every mention to "a round-1 fail (score not retained in the released record) with two blocking issues, repaired to a round-2 pass at 90".

**MINOR-4 — "Byte-for-byte" in the abstract and conclusion omits the timestamp exclusion.**
- **Location:** Abstract ("reproduce all 45 output files byte-for-byte across consecutive executions") and Conclusion ("suite determinism 45/45 byte-identical"); the body (Section 7.3) correctly says "byte-for-byte with timestamps excluded", and `suite_determinism.json` confirms only `executed_at` is excluded.
- **Fix:** Add "(timestamps excluded)" to abstract and conclusion, or define the determinism claim once, precisely, in both.

**MINOR-5 — The released baseline record contradicts itself about the FE-official arm.**
- **Location:** Table 5 (`tab:ablation`) row "FE official code (GLM), T2-contrib features ... 5/6"; released `results/benchmark/baselines.json`.
- **Evidence:** `baselines.json` summary reports `fe_official_prompts_strict: "5/6"` with per-case `fe_official` blocks, but the same file also carries `"fe_official_code": {"note": "official-FE-code run block not present; rebuild via baselines/FaultExplainer replication"}`. Whether the 5/6 arm ran *official FE code* (as the table says) or *official FE prompts* over a local feature extractor (as the key name `fe_official_prompts` and the note suggest) cannot be settled from the released record alone. This is exactly the kind of provenance ambiguity the paper's own C3 contract exists to prevent.
- **Fix:** Reconcile the artifact (one authoritative provenance block: what code, what prompts, what features, what model) and align the table's row label with it.

**MINOR-6 — The leakage sentinel's PASS verdict is not persisted; and `metrics.json`'s `calibrated` field uses different caps than the rubric.**
- **Location:** Section 6.1 (C2: "the leakage sentinel returns a PASS verdict before any metric is accepted"); Table 6 ("Ceiling compliance ... two exceed"); released `metrics.json`.
- **Evidence:** (a) `check-leakage.mjs` is exit-gated in `run-benchmark-pipeline.mjs` (step 1.0) but prints its PASS to console only; `repro_report.json` has no leakage entry, so a reader cannot re-verify the sentinel verdict from artifacts alone. (b) `metrics.json` marks tep_d01 `calibrated: true` at confidence 0.91 (agent-side caps: no DETERMINED cap), while the rubric deducts 15 R4 points against the machine 0.90 gate. The paper explains this two-cap-set gap narratively, but the released machine record silently disagrees with the paper's compliance table, which will confuse artifact-checking readers.
- **Fix:** (a) Persist the sentinel verdict into `repro_report.json`; (b) emit both compliance counts in `metrics.json` (agent-side caps and machine 0.90 gate) with a one-line legend.

**MINOR-7 — n=9/n=12 with one repeat: adequately disclosed, but the revision should capitalise on the pre-registered repeats.**
- **Location:** Section 9 ("Statistical power and single-execution evidence") — the caveats are exemplary; this is a request, not an error.
- **Evidence:** All CIs are correctly computed; single-execution status, repair-selection effect, and the 1/1 stability base are all disclosed. Still, eleven scenarios have outstanding re-run contracts, and even 2–3 additional seeded re-executions (under the MAJOR-1 fix) would move the stability claim from anecdote to evidence.
- **Fix:** For the revision, add at least the two cheapest re-executions (e.g., one DETERMINED fault, one control) using the strengthened draw protocol; otherwise keep the current framing, which is honest.

## 6. Overclaim scan (abstract / intro / conclusion)

- Abstract: all numbers verified (V1–V3, V8–V10). Two wording issues: "byte-for-byte" (MINOR-4) and the seed guarantee inherited from Section 7.4 (MAJOR-1). "top-k = 9/9" carries the keyword-instrument caveat only in the body (MAJOR-2/MINOR-1).
- Introduction (contributions 3–4): the integrity-audit/re-execution narrative is historical but consistent with the repo's era markers; "the graded runs executed as true per-stage sub-agent dispatches" is corroborated by `execution_mode: "pipeline-agents"` in all 12 gradings.
- Conclusion: "accuracy is not the contribution" — supported by the paper's own ablation and the strongest feature of the paper. "every result backed by an execution proof" — verified (12/12 finalize PASS + event logs pass the log checker per `repro_report.json`).

No numerical overclaim found anywhere; the overclaims are precisely the three wording issues above.

## 7. Recommendation

**Major Revision.**

The evaluation is real, machine-checked, and — rare in this space — every headline number reproduces from the released artifacts, with the pipeline's failures (4/9 Top-1 against its own 9/9 bare-model ablation) reported against interest. I found no fabrication indicator and no statistical error. The revision is major only because (i) the single stability measurement is protected by a selection mechanism weaker than the sentence claiming it (MAJOR-1) and is fixable cheaply for the eleven outstanding re-runs; (ii) the paper's instrument-validity story needs the keyword-sensitivity reporting its own repository anticipates (MAJOR-2); and (iii) a small set of artifact-facing sentences (MINOR-2, -3, -5) must be made exactly true, since this paper's contribution *is* the artifact contract. None of these require new science; all are executable within one revision cycle.
