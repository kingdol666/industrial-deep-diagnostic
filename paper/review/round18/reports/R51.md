## 4. Colour, hatch, and grayscale robustness

- Okabe–Ito palette used consistently; the briefing's colour convention holds in every figure:
  IDD dark blue = determined; hatched sky = capped CS ranked (Figs 3a, 4, 8 col 1); amber =
  capped CS unranked (Figs 3a, 4, 6, 8); green = FE hits/control cells (Figs 3b, 6, 7, 8); grey
  = FE/unscored (Figs 3b, 6, 7, 8). Hatch patterns render cleanly at print size and are visible
  in the 110 dpi renders on all hatched bars/cells.
- Grayscale caveat (cosmetic): hatched-sky (#56B4E9, luma≈158) vs hatched-grey (#8F8F8F, 143),
  and amber (≈162) vs sky, are close under grayscale conversion. No misreading is possible in
  the current build only because every coloured bar/cell also carries a text label ("CS 0.65",
  "normal 0.78", "hit", "no"). Keep those labels if the palette is ever changed.
