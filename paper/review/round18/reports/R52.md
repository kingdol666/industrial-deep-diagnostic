## 1. Summary of the paper

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage agentic diagnosis pipeline for industrial root-cause
analysis (ontology → deterministic statistics → competing-hypothesis reasoning → physical
verification → gated reporting), with L1–L7 evidence grading, a three-state verdict protocol
(DETERMINED / COMPETING_SET / NEEDS_DATA), bounded repair governance, and a machine-checked
evaluation framework (truth isolation, fingerprinting, stage-authored artifacts, gate-graded
scoring). It reports a 12-scenario benchmark (SKAB/TEP/IndPenSim; 9 faults + 3 controls):
Top-1 6/9 (CI [35.4, 87.9]; 5/9 pre-revision keywords), TEP subset 5/6 (4/6 pre-revision),
IDV3 stated as a mechanism-level miss, 3/3 controls, and a model-controlled ablation showing
a bare same-model call scores 9/9 — i.e. the pipeline's margin is auditability, not accuracy.

## 2. Abstract promise vs delivery

The abstract (≈245 words by my count; within a 250-word expectation) makes only checkable
claims and — unusually — states its own worst numbers: the IDV3 miss, the 5/9 and 4/6
pre-revision keyword figures, and the bare-LLM 9/9 result all appear in the abstract itself.
Every abstract promise I traced is delivered in the body and matches the released artifacts:
headline numbers (§7.1, Table 7), capped verdicts with named discriminating measurements
(§7.2–7.3, Fig. 4/5), the ablation bound (§8.3, Table 10), zero false alarms, and the rubric
mean. No overclaim found. The phrase "more than the pipeline" for the bare LLM is the correct,
unspun framing.

## 3. Contributions vs delivery

- C1 (quality-gated pipeline): delivered; checkpoints CP-1..CP-9 (Table 3) have mechanical
  predicates that I verified against the released run directories and the finalisation gate.
- C2 (evidence grading/calibration): delivered; the ceiling split between agent-documented
  caps (0.65/0.70/0.50) and the machine gate (0.90 DET / 0.70 CS) is described exactly as
  implemented — including the honest admission that agent-side documents no DET cap.
- C3 (reproducible evaluation framework): delivered; the integrity-audit/shortcut-removal
  history is disclosed (docs/benchmark/AUDIT-2026-09-13.md exists as referenced).
- C4 (12-scenario benchmark with per-fault FE baselines): delivered; the FE comparison is
  carefully quarantined (Register A/B, three stated caveats, no significance test claimed).

## 4. Novelty

Positioning against FaultExplainer is precise (protocol properties i–iii, per-fault table,
PCA-excluded pair). The contemporaneous AgentRCA (July 2026) and Gong et al. are engaged
honestly, with the claim narrowed to what is actually absent elsewhere (calibrated three-state
conclusions + machine-verifiable evaluation contract). §2.4 concedes the intellectual ancestry
(Parasuraman, Heuer, Hill, Chow, OntoCAPE) rather than claiming invention — the right posture.
Novelty is incremental-but-real: a domain-specific, machine-checked instantiation, plus an
evaluation harness whose like I have not seen in the LLM-RCA literature.

## 9. Verdict

I performed ~20 claim-vs-reality checks across numbers, artifact paths, chronology, and
architecture-vs-code, and found **zero mismatches**; the single deviation (median 132 vs
"≈131") is inside the stated approximation. The paper's honesty machinery (negatives in the
abstract, disclosed author-designed instruments, bounded claims) is consistent with what the
released repository actually contains. Remaining weaknesses — single model family, single
recorded execution per scenario, author-designed rubric with 10/12 at ceiling, outstanding
expert anchoring — are all disclosed as limitations with named follow-ups, not discovered by
this review. I sign a favorable report.
