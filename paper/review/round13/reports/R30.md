## 1. Overall assessment

§2–§3 is the paper's strongest section from a systems-engineering standpoint. The
architecture prose is unusually precise — it pre-empts the exact questions a reviewer of
the released tree would ask (package census, dispatch topology, engine-table arithmetic,
per-run RAG degradation) and, in ten focused spot checks, **nine areas verify exactly**.
The system is real, layered as described, and the module-contract presentation
(Table 2 + Table 3 + Figs. 1–2) makes the agent/script split and the audit plane
unmistakable. I found one factual misattribution in the §3.2 RAG run-by-run narrative
(tep_d01) and two presentation defects (Table 2 folio collision; caption fragment).

## 2. Claim-vs-reality spot checks (10 performed; ≥3 required)

## 4. Verdict rationale

The architecture narrative survives adversarial verification better than any system paper
I have reviewed in this cycle of submissions: counts, filenames, error codes, dispatch
labels, checkpoint predicates and per-run degradation records are all real and checkable.
The single factual error (tep_d01) is one clause in an otherwise verifiably true
disclosure paragraph, and the two Table 2 defects are mechanical fixes. This is a
minor revision: correct the tep_d01 attribution (either cite the actual record that shows
its degradation or move it to the silent group and adjust the count), repair the Table 2
folio collision and caption fragment, and optionally enlarge Fig. 2's type.
