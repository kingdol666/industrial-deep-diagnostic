# R32 — SKEPTICAL GENERAL REVIEW (Round 13, fresh/blind)

Paper: `paper/main.tex` (74 pp compiled). Reviewed as a whole paper: abstract promise vs delivery,
coherence, novelty, writing, reproducibility/ethics, and — above all — whether the paper's
descriptions match what the released system actually does. All claims below were verified against
the repository; I read nothing under `paper/review/` except the briefing and the page PNGs.

## 1. Abstract (≤250 words; promises vs delivery)

- **Length**: 245 words (counted from the source after LaTeX stripping). Within limit.
- **Promises delivered**: every quantitative statement in the abstract is reproducible from the
  released results: 6/9 Top-1 with Wilson CI [35.4, 87.9] (`results/benchmark/metrics.json`:
  `cdr=0.6667`, `cdr_ci95=[35.42, 87.94]`); top-k two more (8 vs 6); three capped COMPETING_SET
  verdicts (`per_case[*].diagnosis_type`); 3/3 controls, zero false alarms; rubric 97.9
  (`mean_rubric`); bare-LLM 9/9 (`results/benchmark/baselines.json.summary.single_llm_same_digest_ablation`).
- **The 5/9 and 4/6 qualifiers are unspun**: both appear in the abstract as parenthetical
  concessions with a forward pointer to the framework section, and the same qualifiers are carried
  in the contributions, Table 6 caption, Fig. 6/7 captions, and the conclusion. The single most
  unusual and commendable feature: the abstract itself states that a bare call to the same model
  family (9/9) *beats* the pipeline on documented faults, and reframes the contribution as
  auditability rather than accuracy. This matches the released record and is not overstated.

## 3. Novelty vs FaultExplainer and multi-agent-LLM RCA

- Positioning against FaultExplainer is careful and correctly states the three protocol
  asymmetries (candidates in prompt vs none; top-3-with-alias vs strict single verdict;
  PCA-excluded faults scored). FE's 7/11 and 9/11 numbers are transcribed with the alias caveat and
  the paper flags FE's own 8/11 inconsistency rather than silently adopting it.
- AgentRCA (contemporaneous) is cited and differentiated (ranked list, no calibrated confidence,
  no external truth harness).
- The Table 1 exclusivity claim ("IDD is the only system that combines all five") is hedged to
  "published and contemporaneous systems" and survives scrutiny for the elements stated.
- §2.4 (intellectual ancestry) is a model of honest positioning: three-state verdicts mapped to
  Parasuraman/Lee-See, ACH to Heuer, the anti-spurious filter to Hill, abstention to Chow,
  ontology layer to OntoCAPE — with the contribution explicitly framed as "machine-checked
  instantiation, not invention". A skeptical reader is left with a modest but real novelty claim:
  the machine-verifiable evaluation contract and evidence-graded audit trail, not the accuracy.

## 7. Ethics / honesty ledger

Prominent negatives are present and prominent: the ablation shows the pipeline loses to a bare
call on accuracy (abstract, §6.3, §9, conclusion); the single verdict-type compliance miss is
analysed rather than narrated as success; IDV3 is a mechanism-level miss; the rubric is conceded
to be author-designed with ten of twelve scenarios at ceiling; single-execution evidence and the
repair-loop selection effect are disclosed with counts; the IDV14 12-min vs 30-min period
discrepancy across stability sessions is disclosed and used to bound the signature claim; the
fabrication audit is part of the released history; the seeded re-test draw landing on a scenario
with too few runs is reported as an outstanding contract, not papered over; the bare-LLM arm's
missing TEP control is flagged as an execution-coverage gap in Table 8 and Fig. 8. This is among
the most self-incriminating (and therefore credible) honesty postures I have reviewed.

Residual concerns (not blocking): n=9 with single executions gives wide intervals the paper
acknowledges; one model family/one deployment; the expert-anchoring and filter-disabled/engine
-substitution arms are outstanding; keyword scoring is OR-semantics over author-chosen tokens
(disclosed, with the IndPenSim generic-token caveat stated in §5.2 itself).

## 8. Decision

The paper's description matches the released system everywhere I probed (numbers, artifacts,
chronology, architecture, gates). Its worst facts are in its own abstract. Remaining defects are
one ungrammatical caption fragment, one 0.1 pp figure/table rounding inconsistency, and
tightenable repetition. **Minor revision.**
