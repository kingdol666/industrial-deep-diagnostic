## Blocking defects

**MF-1 (p17, Table 2).** Table 2 plus its 5-line caption exceeds the float page: the last row
("Finalise (9) … `evidence_closure_report.json`") pushes the booktabs bottom rule into the
footer margin, and the folio "17" is printed on top of the rule (verified by 2x zoom crop of
p-17.png; p17 is a float-only page, so the table overflows even with no body text competing).
Fix: shorten the caption by ~2 lines, or drop `\arraystretch`/merge the two HTML rows, or set
the table `\footnotesize` + `\resizebox{\textwidth}` like Tables 1/6. Trivial but hard-blocking
for any copyeditor.

**MF-2 (p37, Fig 3a caption).** The SKAB bar in panel (a) is drawn **hatched** sky
(`make_figures.py:116-118`, black `///` hatch, deliberately matching Figs 4/8), but the caption
says only "(sky blue)" — the promised update to "hatched sky blue" is **not present** in this
build (`main.tex:459`, confirmed on the rendered page). This matters beyond wording: amber and
sky have near-identical grayscale luminance (~163 vs ~164/255), the hatch is the *only* cue that
separates the ranked-CS bar from the amber IDV3 miss segment in a grayscale print, and panel (a)
intentionally has no legend. Fix: one word in `main.tex:459`.

## Should-fix (non-blocking)

**SF-1 (p46 vs p36).** Fig 7 prints the controls row as "3/3 = 100.0% [43.9, 100.0]"; Table 6
prints "[43.8, 100.0]". Exact Wilson lower bound at the released z is 43.8503 → "43.9" is the
correct 1-dp rounding; Table 6 is mis-rounded. Unify (change Table 6 to 43.9 or state 43.85).

**SF-2 (p55, Fig 8).** The saved figure is 6.08 in wide (tight bbox) and is downscaled to the
5.43 in text block (scale 0.893), so the 12 scenario y-labels print at ~6.25 pt (7 pt nominal) —
below the 6.5 pt legibility floor. Cell text (8.1–8.3 pt → ~7.2–7.4 pt), column heads and legend
are fine. Fix: raise y-tick fontsize to ~7.8 or abbreviate labels ("Control: TEP fault-free" →
"TEP control").

**SF-3 (p55, Fig 8 caption).** The caption names dark blue / hatched sky / amber / green / the
PCA ramp / grey "no", but not the **vermillion** "miss / not detected" fill (it appears in col 6
for IDV3) nor the pale-grey "not in protocol scope" fills (col 2 "n/a", col 3 "–"). The in-figure
legend names both, but every other figure's caption is self-contained; keep the convention.

**SF-4 (p40, Fig 5).** The caption correctly states both floor measures (125 samples / 106 runs
vs the pipeline's 39.4% residence) and explicitly refuses to equate them — verified against the
run artifact, which contains both numbers verbatim. Residual nit: the top-panel annotation and
the bottom evidence strip *both* say "lowest quantis… level", so a reader dividing 125/289
(43.3%) still meets an apparent contradiction with 39.4% inside the figure itself. Micro-labels
("bin count" / "residence %") would close it.

**SF-5 (p46, Fig 7).** The controls-row value text "3/3 = 100.0% …" starts immediately at the
right edge of the green marker at 100%; no overlap but effectively zero clearance. Nudge x-offset.
