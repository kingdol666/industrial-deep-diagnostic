# R44 — AEI Blind Review, Round 16 (skeptical general reviewer, fresh/blind)

Paper: `paper/main.tex` (75 pp compiled build, pages `paper/review/round16/pages/p-01..75.png`).
Scope: whole-paper read; abstract promise vs delivery; contributions vs delivery; novelty vs
FaultExplainer and multi-agent LLM RCA; reproducibility posture; structure/writing; and — above
all — whether the paper's description matches what the system actually does. I ran ~15
claim-vs-reality checks against the repository (numbers, artifact paths, chronology, code, and
architecture). Evidence for each is below. I read nothing under `paper/review/` except the
briefing and wrote only this report.

---

## 1. Overall judgement

This is an unusually honest systems paper, and — decisively for me — an unusually *verifiable*
one. Every number, path, timestamp, and architecture claim I checked against the repository
matched, in most cases to the second (journal timestamps, git commit times) or to the exact
integer (per-scenario rubric/judge scores, confidence ceilings, artifact counts). The
prominent negatives are not buried: the abstract itself states the IDV3 mechanism-level miss,
prints both keyword-set results (5/9 pre-revision, 4/6 TEP pre-revision), and concedes that a
bare same-model call beats the pipeline on documented faults (9/9 vs 6/9). The contribution is
accordingly reframed as auditability + calibrated uncertainty + gated process guarantees, and
the paper holds that framing consistently through results, discussion, and conclusion.

- **Abstract (p.1, ≤250 words):** delivers every promise it makes. 6/9 Top-1 with Wilson CI
  [35.4, 87.9] = `results/benchmark/metrics.json` (`top1: 6`, `top1_ci95: [35.42, 87.94]`).
  5/9 pre-revision and 4/6 TEP pre-revision = `results/benchmark/keyword_sensitivity.json`
  (`summary.top1.pre_revision 5/9`, `tep_d01_ac_feed_ratio pre_revision top1 false → calibrated
  true`). IDV3 miss, three capped COMPETING_SET verdicts, zero control false alarms
  (`false_alarms: 0`), bare-LLM 9/9 = `baselines.json summary.single_llm_same_digest_ablation
  "9/9 faults hit"`. Word count sits at ~250 — at the limit, not over, but no headroom.
- **Contributions vs delivery:** all four contributions are delivered and checkable. C1's
  leakage sentinel exists (`scripts/benchmark/check-leakage.mjs`, L1–L4 checks, exit-1 gating);
  C2's 153 fingerprints verified (`repro_report.json dataset_integrity {entries:153,
  verified:153, missing:0}`, status REPRODUCIBLE); C3's provenance labels appear in
  `metrics.json` per-case records (`judge_score_source: "judge-agent-10-criteria"`); C4's
  14-artifact completeness list matches `PIPELINE_ARTIFACTS` in
  `scripts/benchmark/zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs` (~line 288), and the C5 artifact-consistency
  gate output exists (`results/benchmark/consistency_audit.json`).
- **Novelty:** positioning against FaultExplainer is fair and precisely caveated (top-3 +
  alias vs strict single verdict; candidates-in-prompt vs none; PCA-excluded faults; FE's own
  8/11 lenient-regime inconsistency is transcribed and flagged rather than exploited). The
  contemporaneous AgentRCA is engaged honestly (40.0%/61.5% on ten TEP faults, no calibrated
  confidence). Section 2.4 correctly assigns the intellectual ancestry (Parasuraman, Heuer,
  Hill, Chow, OntoCAPE) and claims only the machine-checked instantiation. The genuinely new
  elements are the three-state capped-verdict protocol with gate-enforced ceilings, the
  machine-checked evaluation contract (truth isolation + fingerprinting + stage-authored
  provenance + gate-graded scoring), and the negative-result ablation. This is incremental but
  real, and well suited to AEI's applied audience.
- **Reproducibility/ethics posture:** strong. Data availability, CRediT, funding, COI, and a
  precise GenAI-use declaration are all present (pp. 74–75). The artifact index (Table A2) was
  checked against a canonical run directory — all 20 listed paths exist (see Spot-check 3),
  and the 12 briefs exist under `results/benchmark/briefs/`. The keyword-sensitivity addendum
  exists at exactly the released path with the exact driver named.

## 4. Must-fix (blocking)

None. I could not construct a blocking complaint: every verifiable claim verified, every
promise in the abstract is delivered, and the negatives that matter (IDV3 miss, bare-LLM
9/9, single-execution evidence, author-designed rubric/keywords, n=9 width) are stated in the
abstract, results, and conclusion, not just the discussion.

## 5. Should-fix (non-blocking)

1. Table 4 (p.26): the narrow first column is justified, producing ugly inter-word gaps and
   mid-word hyphenation ("Switch jour- naled", "Calibrated … applied"). Use
   ragged-right for that column. (Accuracy is perfect; this is purely typographic.)
2. Abstract is at the ~250-word ceiling; trimming ~10 words would give production slack.
3. Fig. 8 column-6 header "FE prot. det." is cryptic until the caption's last sentence;
   consider "FE T² trigger" or moving the explanation into the header.
4. Sec. 3.1's three-sentence scoping paragraph (p.8) is one very long sentence-chain; splitting
   it would help readers mapping the package inventory.
5. The `calibrated` field in released grading records means verdict-type compliance, not
   keyword provenance — the paper says so once (p.26); a footnote in Table 7's caption would
   prevent misreading of the released JSONs.
6. Table 1's uniqueness claim ("IDD is the only system that combines all five") is hedged in
   the text ("among published and contemporaneous systems") but not in the table itself; add
   the qualifier to the caption.

## 6. Verdict

Publishable in AEI as-is. The paper's central risk — describing a system that does not do what
it says — is decisively refuted by the repository: I attempted roughly fifteen falsification
attempts across numbers, paths, timestamps, code, and architecture and every one confirmed the
paper. The honesty devices (unspun 5/9 and 4/6 qualifiers, named mechanism-level miss, bare-LLM
ablation above the pipeline, disclosed author-designed instruments, superseded batches shown
rather than hidden) are exactly the "honest negatives" posture AEI asks for, and the released
artifact chain makes the evaluation auditable by any reader.
