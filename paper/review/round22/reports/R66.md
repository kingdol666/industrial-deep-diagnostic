## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually verifiable systems paper. Nearly every architectural assertion in §2–§3 is
checkable against a file in the repository, and in every one of my spot checks the paper's
statement survived contact with the ground truth — including several places (execution-form
markers, RAG degradation taxonomy, keyword-calibration chronology, run-dir UTC numerals) where
a sloppier paper would have over-claimed. The §5 execution-form disclosure is the honest core
of the paper: inline stage execution, the shared-session corollary, the 5a∥5b serialization,
and the driver-runs-stages-directly statement in §6.4 are all disclosed crisply and all match
the released event logs. Verdict: **minor revision** — no blocking inaccuracy found in my
focus areas; a handful of small precision/labeling items should be fixed.
