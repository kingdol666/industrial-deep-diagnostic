## 3. Float discipline

All 8 figures and all 12 tables are top-aligned (float pages are content-at-top via `\@fptop=0pt`). `placeins [section]` confines floats to sections, which produces: float-only pages p.17, p.18, p.72, p.73 (top-aligned, blank lower halves) and two genuine section-end stubs — **p.42** (half page of text, section ends) and **p.55** (4 lines, then blank, before Fig 8 flushes on p.56). Judgement: acceptable as preprint typography — the preamble discloses the trade-off, floats never separate from their section, and production re-flow will absorb the stubs; p.55 is the ugliest artifact (4 lines on an empty page) but is a consequence of keeping Fig 8 inside Section 8, which reviewers should prefer over floats drifting across sections.

**Blocking: none.** The figure programme is internally consistent, caption-faithful, data-faithful (every printed number traced to released artifacts or recomputed), colour-blind-safe with grayscale-robust hatching, and all printed figure text is ≥ ~7 pt (well above the 6.5 pt floor).
