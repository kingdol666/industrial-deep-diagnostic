## 5. Uncertainty quantification — Wilson CIs

All proportions use Wilson 95% intervals at z=1.96, stated as "the released scorer's constant";
`scripts/benchmark/aggregate.mjs:28` indeed defines `wilson(k, n, z = 1.96)`. I recomputed every
interval the paper prints, and all seven match to one decimal:

| k/n | paper | my recompute (z=1.96) |
|---|---|---|
| 6/9 Top-1 | [35.4, 87.9] | [35.4, 87.9] |
| 8/9 top-k | [56.5, 98.0] | [56.5, 98.0] |
| 6/6 determined precision | [61.0, 100.0] | [61.0, 100.0] |
| 3/3 controls | [43.8, 100.0] | [43.8, 100.0] |
| 5/6 TEP subset | [43.6, 97.0] | [43.6, 97.0] |
| 7/11 FE GPT-4o | [35.4, 84.8] | [35.4, 84.8] |
| 9/11 FE o1-preview | [52.3, 94.9] | [52.3, 94.9] |

At the (incorrect) z=1.645 the 6/9 interval would be [39.8, 85.8], so the printed numbers
genuinely are z=1.96, not a normal-approximation slip. Fig. 7 (forest plot) prints the same
intervals and labels them z=1.96; Fig. 4/Table 7 use them consistently; the "controls 3/3"
interval [43.8,100.0] appears identically in text, table, and figure. The paper correctly refuses
a significance test across systems with different scenario sets and states so wherever Register A
is invoked.

## 6. Cost statistics — recomputed, including the even-n trap

Event-log spans (first→last timestamp) over the twelve canonical runs:
55.6, 65.9, 69.4, 74.7, 81.1, 86.6, 131.5, 136.5, 141.1, 146.4, 217.4, 220.1 minutes.
Range 55.6–220.1 → printed "56–220 minutes" (rounded, acceptable). n=12 is even, so the median is
the average of the 6th and 7th order statistics: (86.6 + 131.5)/2 = 109.05 → printed
"median ≈109". This is the correct even-n convention (a naive single-middle-value read would have
given ≈87); the paper's number is right. The figure "one to nearly four hours" (§9) is consistent.
Repetition in §6.4, §8.1, and §9 is internally consistent.

## 10. Verdict

Every number I could recompute — headline counts, CIs, chronology timestamps, sensitivity
outcomes, cost statistics, cap enforcement, marker counts, baseline arm results, determinism and
stability tallies — reproduces exactly from the released artifacts. The paper's disclosure
discipline (pre-revision qualifiers everywhere, honest miss on IDV3, self-defeating ablation
reported prominently, circularity of gate-enforced metrics stated) is well above the norm for
this area. I found no blocking statistical or methodological defect.

MUST-FIX: none.

SHOULD-FIX (polish):
1. §6.4/§8.1: cost range printed as "56–220 min" from raw spans 55.6–220.1 — print one decimal
   (or state rounding) so event-log re-derivation is exact.
2. Table 7: "Honest capped COMPETING_SET 3/9" and "Ceiling compliance 9/9" rows carry no CI while
   all other proportions do; add intervals or a one-line note that these are gate-determined
   counts, not estimates.
3. Fig. 5: the in-panel "125 rows · 106 runs" vs the 39.4% residence metric would benefit from a
   one-line reconciliation of denominators (125/289 ≈ 43.3% ≠ 39.4%) to preempt misreading.
4. `keyword_sensitivity.json` cites grader logic as zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs:366-390; pin the
   exact commit hash of the referenced script so the line range cannot drift.
5. §7.4: consider a small table for the era-stratified agreement (12/12 + 3/3 → 15/15) instead of
   prose only; the stratification is the honest part and deserves structural prominence.
6. Abstract: the qualifier chain is dense; a pointer to a single "differences between keyword
   sets" summary sentence in §5 (which exists) via a named shorthand would aid lay readers.
