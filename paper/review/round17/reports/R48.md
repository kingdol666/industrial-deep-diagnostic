# R48 — Skeptical General Review (Round 17, blind)

Paper: "Trustworthy agentic diagnosis for industrial processes: evidence-graded competing-hypothesis
reasoning evaluated against per-fault literature baselines" (main.tex, 75 pp compiled).
Reviewer role: AEI generalist, fresh eyes, claim-vs-reality focus. Read: full tex, rendered pages
(abstract p.1, Figs. 1–2 on pp.13/15, Table 4 on p.26, Figs. 3–5 on pp.37/40/41, Figs. 6–7 on
pp.46/47, Fig. 8 on p.56), and repository ground truth. Nothing under paper/review/ read except the
briefing and page images.

---

## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually verifiable systems paper. I attempted to break it: I checked more than 30
claims — headline and per-scenario numbers, artifact paths, chronology stamps, RAG-channel states,
architecture inventory, execution-form disclosures, repair governance — against the released
repository. Every check matched except one cost claim (Section 7, see MUST-FIX 1) and one
imprecise sentence about the `harness_mode` field (SHOULD-FIX 2). The paper's most striking
property is that it volunteers and correctly reports the facts most damaging to it: a bare LLM
call beats the pipeline on its own benchmark (9/9 vs 6/9), one scenario's Top-1 hit rests entirely
on an author-added keyword synonym, one grading batch ran minutes before the keyword switch and was
superseded, the rubric is author-designed and saturates, and the stability evidence is mostly from
an earlier pipeline version. As a skeptical reader, I could not find a place where the description
diverges from what the system actually does.

That said, the empirical base is thin: 12 scenarios, one model family, one harness, one recorded
execution per scenario, single author, author-designed grading instruments, no human-expert
anchoring. The paper states all of this plainly and bounds its claims accordingly, which is why I
can sign a favorable report at minor-revision level rather than reject. The contribution is honest
engineering methodology (a machine-checked evaluation contract) plus a modest, honestly-bounded
accuracy result — not a modeling advance.

## 2. Abstract (≤250 words; promises vs delivery)

- Length: 247 words (main.tex:56) — within the 250 limit.
- The IDV3 miss is stated as a miss ("the ninth (TEP IDV3) is reported as a mechanism-level miss").
- Pre-revision numbers are given unspun: "5/9 under the pre-revision keyword set" and TEP
  "4/6 under the pre-revision keyword set" both appear in the abstract.
- The negative ablation result is in the abstract itself: a bare same-model call "resolves 9/9
  faults---more than the pipeline". This is a prominent negative, correctly placed.
- Every promised element (nine-stage orchestration, L1–L7 grading, four-condition filter,
  machine-checked framework) is delivered in the body. The 6 DET + 3 CS (2 true-ranked, 1 miss)
  arithmetic across 9 faults is internally consistent.
- Minor: the final sentence "Internal consistency is machine-verified" is technically about the
  artifact-consistency gate (C5), not external validation; the body says this clearly
  (Section 5, C5), the abstract slightly compresses it. Acceptable.

## 3. Contributions vs delivery, novelty

- Contribution 1 (gated pipeline), 2 (evidence grading/calibration), 3 (evaluation framework),
  4 (12-scenario benchmark): all delivered; Sections 3–8 match the claims module by module
  (verified against skill packages, engine code, rubric code, run artifacts).
- Novelty positioning vs FaultExplainer: fair and precise (candidate list + alias acceptance vs
  strict single verdict; PCA-excluded IDV3/IDV4 scored by IDD; FE's own 8/11-with-failing-faults
  inconsistency is transcribed with a disclaimer, not hidden). The per-fault comparison is
  repeatedly framed as descriptive; no significance test is claimed. Correct posture.
- Novelty vs multi-agent LLM RCA (AgentRCA, Gong et al., CausalKGPT, FD-LLM): the positioning
  table (Table 1) is defensible; the paper's own honesty about its ancestry (Heuer ACH, Hill's
  criteria, Chow's reject option, Parasuraman/Lee & See — Section 2.4) is exemplary and rare.
  The genuinely new element is the machine-checked evaluation contract (truth isolation,
  fingerprinting, stage-authored provenance, gate-graded scoring, execution-proof gating); the
  diagnosis methodology itself is an instantiation. For AEI's applied audience this is an
  appropriate contribution profile.

## 5. Prominent negatives — are they prominent enough?

Yes. The bare-LLM 9/9 > pipeline 6/9 result is in the abstract, contributions-adjacent text,
Section 8.3, the discussion, and the conclusion. The verdict-type miss, the keyword dependence of
IDV1, the IDV14 period-estimate instability across sessions (≈12 min vs ≈30 min), the one
weak mechanism-level agreement in stability, the rubric's saturation (10/12 at ceiling), the
one-directional verdict typing, and the absent ablation arms (filter-disabled, engine-substitution)
are all stated in the body. I found no buried negative.

The remaining scientific gap the paper cannot fix by wording: since the bare call already wins on
accuracy and the filter/engine arms are unexecuted, the pipeline's component-level value is
unattributed — the paper says exactly this ("Attribution ... still requires the filter-disabled and
engine-substitution arms, which remain future work"). A "bare LLM + the deterministic gates" arm
would isolate the machinery's value better than the current design; I ask for this as future work,
not as a blocking item, since the paper pre-registered the missing arms.

## 8. Decision

The one true factual slip (cost) and the `harness_mode` wording are one-line fixes. Everything
else I would normally demand under "honest negatives" is already in the paper. I sign off at
MINOR_REVISION.

MUST-FIX (blocking):
1. pp.~35/46/51 (Sections 6.4, 8.1, 9): replace "roughly one hour of agent execution per scenario"
   with the measured span (median ≈131 min, range 56–220 min across the 12 canonical event logs),
   or define the metric; as written it understates cost ~2× — evidence: event-log timestamps of
   the 12 canonical run dirs.
2. p.26 Table 4 right column wraps badly ("before re-executions"; time ranges split) — repack
   (wider column or shorter labels).

SHOULD-FIX (non-blocking):
1. Section 5 "Execution form": state precisely that the three 09-15 canonical logs use the
   `inline_execution`/disclosure form and carry no `harness_mode` field (only the 09-14 logs do);
   the current sentence implies all runs carry the field.
2. Fig. 3(a): annotate the SKAB bar so "0/2" cannot be skimmed as failure (e.g. "0/2 Top-1,
   2 CS with true mechanism ranked").
3. Break up the 200+-word clarification paragraph in Section 3.1 and the stability paragraph in
   Section 8.5.
4. Add the "bare LLM + deterministic gates" arm to the pre-registered ablation list — it would
   attribute value to the machinery better than the two currently-missing arms alone.
5. Abstract's final sentence: consider "the released evaluation's internal consistency is
   machine-verified" to preempt an external-validation reading.
6. State the TEP-subset CI basis (n=6) next to 5/6 in Table A1 as done in Fig. 7.

PUBLISH-READY: Not yet — after the cost-claim correction and the two cosmetic fixes this is an
acceptable, unusually trustworthy systems paper for AEI.
