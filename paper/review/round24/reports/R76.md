## 7. Weaknesses (none blocking, all disclosed by the paper)

1. n=9 with CI [35.4, 87.9]: the headline accuracy is statistically weak, and the ablation shows
   the bare model beats the pipeline on accuracy. The paper converts this into its central,
   honest thesis (value = auditability/calibration/gates), which I accept — but an AEI reader
   should understand the accuracy contribution is nil by the paper's own showing.
2. One author + one model family + one deployment; keywords/rubric/verdict-sets are
   author-designed; independent expert anchoring outstanding. Disclosed repeatedly; still the
   single biggest scientific limitation.
3. The inline execution form means the benchmark did not exercise the sub-agent dispatch
   architecture that §3 sells; the disclosure is prominent (contribution 4 + §5), and the
   artifact contracts are unchanged, but the architecture's communication claims remain
   demonstrated only by the production harness.
4. The film-line deployment (§9) is anonymised and unverifiable; it is properly labelled as
   observed-in-one-deployment and separated from projected properties.

The paper is unusually well-aligned with its released evidence: eleven independent probes,
including a second-level chronology reconstruction and code-level architecture checks, found
zero mismatches. Negatives are prominent, ceilings and circularities are stated plainly, and the
figures are print quality. Remaining issues are editorial.
