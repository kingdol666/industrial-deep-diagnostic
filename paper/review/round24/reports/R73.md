### 2.7 Top-k definition precision — **the one real methods defect**
The paper gives two different definitions of the top-k metric:
- §6.2 (Metrics, p. 30): "**top-k (k=3**; because the protocol mandates at least three *generated*
  hypotheses, top-3 credit approximates candidate-list membership…)" — a pre-elimination,
  generated-hypothesis-set definition.
- Table 7 caption (p. 37): "The top-k row counts scenarios whose **surviving-hypothesis set**
  names the true mechanism (equivalent to Top-1 for single-survivor determined verdicts)" — a
  post-elimination definition.
The released grader (`zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs:366-390`) computes the second variant (keyword
match within primary finding + surviving hypotheses), which is also the definition under which
8/9 is the right number (IDV3's survivors exclude the true mechanism → topk=false; under the
§6.2 wording its ≥3 generated hypotheses would likely include the true mechanism before
elimination, giving a different, larger number). §6.1's phrase "places the true mechanism among
the candidates in an additional two scenarios" is consistent with the surviving-set reading but
the word "candidates" is ambiguous. A headline metric (8/9, plotted in Fig 7) must have exactly
one definition in the paper. → MUST-FIX 1.

## 4. Must-fix / should-fix

### MUST-FIX (blocking)
1. **§6.2 (Metrics, p. 30) vs Table 7 caption (p. 37) — two conflicting definitions of top-k.**
   §6.2 defines top-k over the ≥3 *generated* hypotheses ("k=3 … top-3 credit approximates
   candidate-list membership"); Table 7 (and the released grader,
   `scripts/benchmark/zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs:366-390`) define/compute it over the
   *surviving*-hypothesis set plus the primary finding. The two definitions can yield different
   counts (IDV3 is a top-k miss only under the computed definition). Rewrite the §6.2 sentence to
   state the computed definition exactly. One-line text fix; blocking because 8/9 is a headline
   number plotted in Fig 7.

### SHOULD-FIX (non-blocking)
1. Table 7: the determined-verdict-precision row prints a Wilson CI ([61.0, 100.0]) for a
   self-selected, non-independent conditional subset; the caption already discloses both issues —
   consider dropping the CI for this row rather than quantifying an ill-defined estimand.
2. Fig 3 caption (p. 38): add the global "5/9 pre-revision" qualifier for uniformity with Fig 7
   (only the TEP 4/6 qualifier appears, over bars that sum to the 6/9 headline).
3. §6.2/§5: state once that the journal `calibrated` field's false values on skab_valve1_1 mean
   verdict-type non-compliance, so readers do not misread the sweep's `calibrated:false` rows as
   keyword non-provenance (the paper says this in §5; a parenthetical in §6.1 would prevent
   misreading of Table 7's 8/9 row).
4. §5 execution-form sentence: the "eight runs / three notes" split depends on how the SKAB-valve
   run (which carries both a structured field and the quoted reason prose) is classified; a
   released marker inventory (one line per run) would end any ambiguity.
5. §7.4: the 15/15 stability headline is dominated by pre-discipline-era pairs (12 of 15); the
   split is disclosed, but leading the sentence with the current-era 3/3 (then the 12/12 early
   era) would better match the evidence's weight.
6. Fig 7: the controls row CI label "[43.8, 100.0]" collides with the axis end; nudge the label
   offset (caption already apologises for the overrun).

## 5. Verdict

The empirical machinery of this paper is unusually sound: every one of the ten claim clusters I
recomputed from raw artifacts (CIs, cost median with even n, keyword sensitivity under the
grader's own matching logic, chronology, caps, markers, baselines, stability, rubric, repair
counts) matched the paper exactly, the uncertainty quantification is correct and consistently
z=1.96, the baselines are fair (same-model controls, coverage gaps disclosed), and the negatives
(bare-LLM superiority on accuracy, IDV3 miss, single-execution caveat, author-designed
instruments) are disclosed prominently. The single blocking defect is a metric-definition
inconsistency (top-k) that is fixable in one sentence; a handful of caption/uniformity nits
remain. This is a minor revision.
