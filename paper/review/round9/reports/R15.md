## 7. Verdict

The six data figures are data-driven, internally consistent, and every one of the ≥6 number
spot-checks (plus the case study and all CIs) reproduces exactly from the released JSONs — no
figure–text disagreement was found anywhere. What keeps the paper from camera-ready is purely
presentational: two badly under-filled float pages (one at 27%), two more under-filled float
pages, one caption with no colour key on a colour-heavy figure, and one undeclared two-hue
coding. All are one-pass fixes with no content risk.

**Verdict: MINOR_REVISION.** PUBLISH-READY: no — fix the float-page repack (p-69, p-17, p-39,
p-41) and add the two missing colour declarations (Fig 6 caption, Fig 4 green bar) before
acceptance.
