# R16 — Skeptical General Review (blind, fresh) — Round 9

Paper: "Trustworthy agentic diagnosis for industrial processes: evidence-graded competing-hypothesis
reasoning evaluated against per-fault literature baselines" (main.tex, 72 pp, compiled build verified:
main.log "Output written on main.pdf (72 pages").

Reviewer role: whole-paper read — abstract/intro promises vs delivery, coherence, novelty, writing,
reproducibility/ethics posture, and above all whether the paper's description matches what the system
actually does. I verified claims directly against the repository (code, skill definitions, released
results and run directories), not against the paper's own narrative.

---

## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually verifiable systems paper. The central design decision — every headline claim is
backed by a machine-checkable artifact in a released evaluation repository — is not rhetorical: I
re-derived or re-read the numbers behind the abstract, the results tables, the ablation, the
stability section, and the keyword-sensitivity disclosure, and everything I checked matches the
released records. Equally important, the paper repeatedly bounds its own claims against its own
evidence: the bare-LLM ablation (9/9 vs the pipeline's 6/9) is stated in the abstract itself, the
keyword calibration that produces the 6/9 is disclosed together with a re-scoring showing Top-1
drops to 5/9 under the pre-revision keyword sets, the three capped COMPETING_SET verdicts are
counted as a compliance miss where the scenario expectation was DETERMINED-only, and the
outstanding re-test execution contract is reported as a gap rather than papered over. I found no
overclaiming of substance. The remaining problems are small, local, and fixable in one pass.

## 2. Focus-area findings

### 2.1 Abstract, contributions, conclusion — promise vs delivery
- Abstract promises: 9-stage orchestration, L1–L7 evidence grading, four-condition anti-spurious
  filter, machine-checked evaluation framework, 12 scenarios / 9 faults / 3 controls, 6/9 Top-1 with
  Wilson CI, two more ranked in candidates, 3 capped verdicts naming the missing measurement, zero
  false alarms, TEP subset 5/6, bare-LLM 9/9 bounding all accuracy claims, machine-verified internal
  consistency. Every one of these is delivered and verified (Section 3 below). No promise in the
  abstract is left undelivered; no accuracy claim survives without the ablation bound attached.
- Contribution 4's phrasing "matching the strongest o1-preview baseline on the shared detectable
  faults" is fair (both 4/4 on the shared detectable subset) and is properly caveated in §8.1
  (different base models, different grading regimes, "we read the per-fault table descriptively, not
  as a superiority claim").
- Conclusion repeats the bounds explicitly (single executions, author-designed instruments,
  outstanding expert anchoring, 9/9 vs 6/9). Honest.

### 2.2 Bare-LLM 9/9 ablation — honesty of the accuracy bounding
The paper does not hide the inconvenient result; it leads with it. The abstract states the bare call
resolves "9/9 faults---more than the pipeline", Table 8 shows IDD full pipeline 6/9 below the
single-LLM row, §8.3 says the bare call is "not merely equal to the full pipeline but ahead of it",
and the discussion/conclusion convert the pipeline's claimed margin to auditability/calibration/
process guarantees rather than accuracy. I verified 9/9 in results/benchmark/baselines.json
(strict_top1_hit true for all nine faults, zero false alarms on the two controls the arm covered,
with an explicit released note that not every control was run bare). This is the correct, honest
framing and it is consistent everywhere it appears.

### 2.3 Keyword-sensitivity result — stated without spin
Paper (§5 C1): the calibration shifts "exactly one scenario in each direction: TEP IDV1 becomes a
keyword hit only under the calibrated set (its sole hit is the added synonym), so Top-1 is 5/9
pre-revision versus 6/9 calibrated, while top-k stays 8/9". Ground truth
(results/benchmark/keyword_sensitivity.json): summary.top1 pre_revision 5/9, calibrated 6/9;
topk 8/9 under both; changed scenarios tep_d01_ac_feed_ratio (top1 false→true only under
calibrated) and tep_d03_hard (topk true→false). The paper explicitly attributes the 6/9 to the
calibrated set rather than implying keyword-robustness, discloses the keys are author-designed
(§5, §9), and discloses the timing (calibration applied once, uniformly, before final aggregation;
one pre-calibration grading superseded; pinned at commit 221d061). No spin detected. The released
addendum is itself labelled "post-hoc analysis, not part of the canonical grading".

### 2.4 Prominent negatives — present and visible
- 3 capped COMPETING_SET verdicts: reported as 3/9 in Table 6, analysed in §7.2/§7.3/§9; the SKAB
  valve capped verdict is counted as the benchmark's single verdict-type compliance miss (rubric 85,
  R4 fail verified in rubric.json with allowed=["DETERMINED"], got COMPETING_SET) "rather than
  narrated as a success" (§9). IDV3 is called "a mechanism-level miss" in Fig 3's own caption.
- Bare-LLM beating the pipeline: see 2.2.
- Outstanding re-test contract: §8.4 reports the seeded draw (round 4, seed 149290398, u=0.803782468,
  TEP IDV11 — verified byte-exact in retest_selection.json), the INSUFFICIENT-RUNS status, and the
  outstanding execution contract; the round-4 draw is the third draw selecting IDV11, exactly as
  stated.
- Additional disclosed negatives: fabricated-artifact shortcut found and removed (C4, §9), TEP briefs
  rewritten after a ground-truth-phrasing audit, degenerate self-declared judge score replaced,
  IDV14 period estimate not stable across sessions, RAG channel mostly degraded/bypassed, no
  human-expert comparison, one model family / one deployment. This density of adverse disclosure is
  well beyond field norm.

### 2.5 Novelty vs prior work
Related work is current and specific. FaultExplainer is characterised at protocol level (two prompt
regimes, 7/11 GPT-4o and 9/11 o1-preview with candidates and alias acceptance; 8/11 lenient
no-candidate regime — with the internal inconsistency of FE's own 8/11 figure openly flagged and
transcribed as reported), and the three protocol properties that govern comparability are stated
wherever the comparison is invoked. Contemporaneous agentic RCA (AgentRCA, 40.0/61.5 on ten TEP
faults), multi-agent QA-only work (Gong et al.), and four works from the target journal are
positioned; Table 1's differentiation (calibrated three-state conclusions + machine-verifiable
evaluation contract) is defensible, and §2.4 candidly assigns the intellectual ancestry (Heuer ACH,
Hill's criteria, Chow's reject option, Parasuraman/Lee&See) to prior work — the claimed novelty is a
domain-specific, machine-checked instantiation, not an invention. For an applied engineering-
informatics venue this is an appropriate novelty claim. The bare-LLM result correctly forecloses any
implicit accuracy-novelty claim.

### 2.6 Reproducibility and ethics posture
- Released-artifact index (Table A2) matches disk: results/benchmark/ contains metrics.json,
  rubric.json, gradings/ (12), briefs/ (12), baselines.json, keyword_sensitivity.json,
  suite_determinism.json, stability_report.json, retest_selection.json, journal.jsonl,
  benchmark_report_en.md, baseline_fe_answers/, fe_official_prompts/, dataset_manifest.json,
  repro_report.json — all present; run directories contain the indexed per-run artifacts.
- The 14-artifact completeness gate (Table 4) matches the released grader list exactly
  (PIPELINE_ARTIFACTS, 14 items, plus the <5,120 B HTML rejection).
- Determinism (45/45 byte-identical, timestamps excluded, verdict DETERMINISTIC), stability (15/15
  within-era pairs = 12 pre-discipline case-eras + 3 current; 40 run directories — both numbers
  verified), seeded re-test draw, and the audit record docs/benchmark/AUDIT-2026-09-13.md all exist
  as described.
- Back matter complete: CRediT, competing interest, funding, data availability (supplementary
  repository; Zenodo DOI promised at acceptance), and an explicit GenAI declaration covering both
  writing assistance and the fact that the benchmark executions were produced by the IDD pipeline
  itself. Appropriate and above-board.

### 2.7 Structure, writing, notation, length
- The four numbering schemes (pipeline Steps, agent Phases, R1–R8, method stages) are a known
  hazard; the paper mentions them once (§3.3) but never enumerates them, and the R1–R8 and
  method-stage schemes are barely visible in the paper itself (R1–R8 appears only in Table 4; the
  method-stage scheme does not appear at all). The parenthetical is therefore underspecified for a
  reader.
- Verdict-state notation is declared once (§4.1/§4.2: small-capped DETERMINED/COMPETING_SET/
  NEEDS_DATA are protocol terms) and used consistently; \textsc{det}/\textsc{cs}/\textsc{nd} in
  Eq. (2) is legible shorthand. Metrics are defined before use; Wilson CIs are used throughout and I
  recomputed four of them (6/9, 8/9, 6/6, 3/3, 5/6, 7/11, 9/11) — all correct.
- Figures are print-quality (Okabe-Ito palette, self-contained captions, annotations read from
  released artifacts). Two layout blemishes: a stranded fragment "phasis." on p. 35 (the sentence
  "Three observations deserve emphasis" is split around the Figure 3 float page), and open vs filled
  markers in Fig 3(b) are tight at print size.
- Length: 72 pp in elsarticle 12pt preprint format is long; §9's deployment-prospects material and
  parts of §2/§9 overlap and could be tightened without losing content. Not disqualifying for AEI,
  which tolerates thorough system papers, but a reduction of ~10–15% would help.

## 4. Must-fix (blocking)

1. p. 50, §8.3 (Fig 8 discussion): "the controls pass everywhere" contradicts the paper's own
   Table 8 ("2 controls" in the bare-LLM arm) and Fig 8 column 2 ("n/a" for the TEP fault-free
   control). Reword to "the covered controls pass" — a one-line factual wording fix.
2. p. 13, §3.3: "the paper's four numbering schemes are independent" — the four schemes are never
   enumerated; only Steps and Phases are introduced, R1–R8 appears once in Table 4, and the
   method-stage scheme appears nowhere. Enumerate the four schemes at first mention or delete the
   count.

## 5. Should-fix

1. p. 35: stranded fragment "phasis." below Fig 3 (paragraph split by the float page) — reflow or
   move the float.
2. Length: consolidate §9 deployment prospects and trim §2/§9 overlap; ~10–15% shorter would sharpen
   the paper.
3. Fig 3(b): increase open/filled marker separation (IDV11 and SKAB-valve rows are hard to read at
   print size).
4. Data availability: consider minting the Zenodo DOI at submission rather than promising it at
   acceptance.
5. §5 C1 is a single very long paragraph carrying the leakage/calibration/sensitivity disclosures;
   split into named sub-items for scanability.
6. Surface per-scenario repair counts in the grading records (currently only in event logs) so the
   "eleven zero-repair, one single-repair" claim is checkable without log parsing.

## 6. Verdict

All accuracy-bearing claims are bounded by the paper's own evidence and every spot check against the
repository matched. The two must-fix items are one-line corrections; nothing blocks publication
after a minor revision.
