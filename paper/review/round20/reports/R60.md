## 1. Overall impression

This is an unusually honest systems paper. The abstract (248 words, within the 250 limit) states the headline result unspun — 6/9 Top-1 with a wide Wilson CI, the pre-revision keyword numbers (5/9; TEP 4/6) printed in the abstract itself, IDV3 explicitly named as a mechanism-level miss, and — remarkably — the model-controlled ablation result that a bare call to the same model family beats the pipeline (9/9 vs 6/9) *in the abstract*, with the paper's claimed residual value repositioned to auditability, calibrated uncertainty, and gated process guarantees. The closing abstract sentence ("Internal consistency is machine-verified against the released execution proofs") is carefully scoped to internal consistency, not external validation. The paper's promises (contributions 1–4) are each delivered in the body, and — the decisive test for this review — **every claim I checked against the released repository matched, without exception** (15+ spot checks below).

The principal weaknesses are presentational and conceptual, not factual: heavy repetition of the pre-revision disclosure, length (75 pp), some sparse figures, a residual self-referentiality in the evaluation machinery that is disclosed but cannot be engineered away, and one unproven causal aside in the FE-official analysis. None of these is blocking.

## 5. Novelty and positioning

Positioning against FaultExplainer is fair and precisely scoped (top-3-with-alias vs strict single verdict; candidates-in-prompt vs none; PCA-excluded faults scored by IDD), and the paper transcribes FE's own inconsistencies (8/11 under lenient grading despite two named failures) rather than hiding them. AgentRCA and Gong et al. are cited and the claim boundary ("none combines real series + mechanism RCA + calibrated three-state + machine-verifiable contract") is defensible given the stated evidence. Section 2.4 (Parasuraman/Lee-See/Heuer/Hill/Chow ancestry) is a candid "instantiation, not invention" statement that strengthens credibility. No superiority claim is made over FE; the comparison is repeatedly labelled descriptive. This is how comparisons should be done.

## 6. Weaknesses (non-blocking)

1. **Repetition.** The pre-revision disclosure "(5/9; TEP subset 4/6)" appears at least nine times (abstract, contribution 4, §5, Table 4 caption context, Table 7 caption, §6.1, Figs 6/7 captions, §7.1, conclusion). Twice (abstract + framework section) suffices; the rest reads defensively.
2. **Self-referential evaluation core.** The rubric, keywords, allowed-verdict sets, and gates are author-designed instruments measuring conformance to the paper's own protocol. The paper discloses this prominently (§6.2 "we state its circularity plainly", §9 "author-designed instruments"), and the truth-comparison (keyword hits against public ground truth) is the only external anchor — with the bare-LLM 9/9 showing the benchmark cannot demonstrate accuracy value. The residual burden on the "trustworthy" title is borne entirely by process guarantees whose independent validation (expert adjudication) is outstanding. Disclosed, but a reader should understand the title's "Trustworthy" as "auditable", which the paper largely earns.
3. **One unproven causal aside (§7.3).** "FE misses IDV4 because its PCA T² features are dominated by feed-composition shifts…" — the miss is verified; the *because* is an interpretation presented as fact. Soften to "plausibly because".
4. **Length and density.** 75 pages with a single-author single-model n=9 evidence base; §3.1's second paragraph is one enormous paragraph packing three scoping clarifications. AEI readers would benefit from an architecture-summary table and tighter §5.
5. **Deployment evidence.** The one operational deployment (film line) is anonymised and not released; the novel-disturbance suite — the only setting where the pipeline could beat recall — is "under construction". The paper says so; the practical case remains open.

## 7. Reproducibility/ethics posture

Data availability promises the repository as supplementary with a Zenodo DOI on acceptance; CRediT, COI, funding, and GenAI declarations are present and appropriately worded (the GenAI statement distinguishes writing assistance from the system under study). The benchmark configuration disclosure (ZCode CLI, GLM family, Sept 2026 snapshot, no temperature control, non-visual VLM degradation, repair counts) is exemplary. Ethics: public datasets, no human subjects, truth isolation documented with a machine-checked sentinel.

## 8. Verdict

Every factual claim I tested reproduces against the released repository — including the unflattering ones. The paper's promises are delivered, its negatives are prominent, and its evaluation contracts are real code, not rhetoric. Remaining issues are editorial (repetition, one causal aside, figure whitespace, length). I sign a favorable report.
