# Reviewer B Report — Domain Expert (LLM-based industrial fault diagnosis & process monitoring)

Manuscript: "Auditable agentic diagnosis for industrial processes: evidence-graded competing-hypothesis reasoning evaluated against per-fault literature baselines" (round 20, blind review).

---

## Paper Summary

The paper presents the Industrial Deep Diagnostic (IDD) pipeline: a nine-stage agentic orchestration that couples deterministic statistics with LLM competing-hypothesis reasoning under an evidence-grading scheme (L1–L7), a four-condition anti-spurious-correlation filter, bounded repair loops, and physical verification. Its methodological contribution is a machine-checked evaluation contract (truth isolation C1, data fingerprinting C2, stage-authored artifacts with provenance C3, gate-graded scoring C4, artifact-consistency gate C5). The benchmark is 12 scenarios over SKAB (2 faults + 1 control), TEP (6 documented faults + control), and IndPenSim (batch 93 + control). Headline results: resolved Top-1 = 4/9 (identity-strict 3/9; joint identifier-contamination floor 0/9), top-k = 9/9 (branch-localized 8/9), zero false alarms on 3 controls (one blind). A same-model ablation shows a bare single call over the pipeline's blinded statistical digest scores 9/9 strict keyword hits versus the pipeline's 4/9 resolved, so the paper explicitly relocates its value claim from accuracy to process qualities (auditability, capped-honest three-state verdicts). A FaultExplainer (FE) per-fault Register-A comparison is reported with disclosed protocol asymmetries; one seeded re-execution (TEP IDV11) reproduced verdict state, actuator, and mechanism class; the deterministic suite arms reproduce 45 run outputs byte-identically.

**Verification performed for this review.** (a) I spot-checked 24 in-text citations against the bibliography and my domain knowledge: downs1993plant, lyman1995plant, chiang2001fault, russell2000delay, bathelt2015revision, rieth2017additional, khan2024faultexplainer, wei2026agentrca, agentic2026review, gong2026harnessing, constantinides2025failuresensoriq, zhou2024causalkgpt, lin2025fdllm, wen2025troubleshooting, xu2026digitaltwin, liu2025spatial, yue2023root, liu2024label, alsaif2024multimodal, merkelbach2024multimodal, vieira2026towards, hendriks2022towards, horch1999simple, hagglund1995control, choudhury2008diagnosis, alfayez2005detection, scott2014input, cemri2025mast, chen2023automatic, goldrick2015development/goldrick2019modern. All resolve to real, correctly attributed works; none appears fabricated; the TEP alias classes (IDV3↔IDV9, IDV4↔IDV11↔IDV14, IDV5↔IDV12↔IDV15) and the FE PCA exclusions (IDV3/4/9/15) match the FE paper as I know it. ## Major Issues

**M1. Every resolved verdict is contaminated by the identifier channel; the committed neutralized-identifier re-run should be executed within this revision, or the headline must lead with the recall-possible tier.**
Anchors: abstract ("Top-1 44.4%—a provisional upper bound…joint worst-case floor is 0/9"); §1 contribution 4; §7 "Prompt-knowledge contamination"; Table 3. The three TEP resolved verdicts (IDV1/7/14) come from run directories that embed the documented mechanism and dynamic form (e.g., `tep_d14_reactor_valve_sticking`), the leakage sentinel explicitly does not cover directory names (§5 C1), and the dispatcher context is a second adjacent channel. Batch 93 — the fourth resolved verdict — is already discounted under the identity-strict reading. Consequently the paper currently has *no* uncontaminated resolved-verdict evidence; top-k 9/9 is design-aided (keywords derive from the same documented cause table) and capped-verdict survivor composition is also recall-exposed. To the authors' credit this is stated exhaustively, and the remedy (hash-id directories, one orchestrating session per scenario, ≈16 h agent time by their own cost accounting) is committed. My concrete fix: **execute the committed neutralized-identifier re-run during revision** (it is cheap relative to the revision cycle and it is the single experiment that converts "recall-possible" into evidence), and report it as an addendum to Table 3 and §7. If it cannot be executed, then Table 3's Top-1 row, the abstract, and Fig. 3 must print the 3/9 identity-strict and 0/9 joint-floor readings as the primary figures, with 4/9 relegated to the disclosed upper-bound register — everywhere, not just in prose.

**M2. The "process qualities, not accuracy" framing survives skeptical reading only as an unvalidated design claim; validate at least one leg of it now.**
Anchors: abstract ("whose correctness anchoring by blinded expert adjudication remains the outstanding validation step"); §6.2 ("nine of the twelve scenarios score at ceiling… correctness claims rest on the truth comparison"); §7 "Inference authorship and provenance" ("architectural properties, not yet measured outcomes"); Table 6 (trust model). I accept the reframing — the ablation is disclosed in the strongest possible form (9/9 vs 4/9 against the paper's own interest), and the honest instrument is exactly what distinguishes this manuscript from most LLM-FDD literature. But as it stands the claimed margins are measured by author-designed instruments (rubric at ceiling for 9/12; keyword sets calibrated on v1-era executions of the same scenarios — instrument contamination, disclosed). Two bounded fixes, either of which materially strengthens the paper: (i) run the blinded expert adjudication on the nine released evidence chains even at n = 2–3 domain experts (the chains are self-contained; §6.5's IDV4 chain is the natural pilot), reporting inter-expert agreement on "is each elimination substantiated by the cited statistic"; or (ii) run the pre-registered mutation-sensitivity pilot on a subset (inject corrupted statistics/wrong eliminations into artifacts and measure which gates catch them). Additionally, §1's contribution list should carry the unvalidated qualifier on contribution 2 (evidence grading/calibration) rather than deferring it to §7 — currently the introduction enumerates it without the caveat that its validation is outstanding.

**M3. The "single-LLM" ablation arm is digest-primed; rename it and bound what 9/9 measures.**
Anchor: §6.3 (iii): "one call to the same model family over the identical blinded brief, whose evidence block is the pipeline's deterministic statistical digest." This arm is not a bare model on data — it receives IDD's own computed signatures (variance ratio 40.1 with frozen means, quantised stepping 633/800, the +6.8σ step). The 9/9 therefore measures "LLM reading a good deterministic digest," which actually *supports* the paper's deterministic/semantic split argument, but the current naming ("Single-LLM, identical blind digest" in Table 5 is accurate; the prose "bare call" is not) invites misreading as "an unaided LLM matches the pipeline." Fixes: (a) use "digest-primed single call" consistently in prose, abstract, and conclusion; (b) add one sentence reporting which digest numbers the released bare answers actually cite (checkable from the verbatim answers already released); (c) pre-register (or run) the true no-digest arm — raw CSV only — since the contamination question applies to it identically and it is the natural companion to the neutralized-identifier re-run in M1.

**M4. Pin the TEP recording provenance for the Register-A comparison.**
Anchors: §6.1 (TEP cites downs1993plant, lyman1995plant, rieth2017additional, bathelt2015revision); §7.1; Fig. 5; Table A1. The paper never states which TEP simulation version/dataset the six fault recordings come from (Rieth's Harvard Dataverse runs? which revision per Bathelt et al.?), nor whether IDD and the FE published numbers are evaluated on the same recordings. FE used its own simulation outputs; if IDD's recordings differ (different revision, different noise realization, different window length), that is one more Register-A asymmetry that belongs beside the already-disclosed candidate/alias/onset asymmetries — and it matters for the IDV3/IDV4 detectability rows of Fig. 5, where detection percentages depend on the recording and window. Fix: one or two sentences in §6.1 naming the exact source and version of each TEP recording (the SHA-256 fingerprints of C2 already pin them — surface the mapping), and one sentence in §7.1 stating whether FE's Table 1 outcomes were obtained on the same recordings.

**M5. The flagship IDV4 energy-balance argument is asserted, not shown.**
Anchor: §6.5: the necessity argument, the "temperature approach below 1.5 K," "coolant inlet above ≈93 °C," and "≈26 K approach observed in the pre-onset data." This case study is the paper's evidence that the reasoning layer adds more than a channel inventory (§7, attribution caveat), so its quantitative core must be checkable. As written, the algebra is unreconstructable: which temperatures enter the balance (reactor, jacket/coolant outlet, the twin thermocouples' +2.0/−2.3σ transient), what heat-removal duty is assumed conserved, and how the 1.5 K threshold follows. Fix: add a short appendix (or supplement memo) with the balance Q = ṁc_p(T_out − T_in) = UA·LMTD written out with the released numbers, deriving the 1.5 K / 93 °C / 26 K quantities, and state the LMTD-redistribution loophole in the same notation. The direction of the argument is physically right (a pure UA loss cannot conserve the observed loop temperatures without an implausibly small pre-existing approach), but a domain reader should be able to verify it in five minutes rather than take it on trust.

**M6. Related-work coverage is good but leans on one review and one contemporaneous preprint; two additions are warranted, and one quoted number needs a verification pointer.**
Anchors: §2.2–2.3, Table 1. The 2023–2026 LLM-FDD/RCA landscape is covered better than in most submissions (FE, AgentRCA, CausalKGPT, FD-LLM, troubleshooting, digital-twin VLM, multi-agent MCQA, multimodal lines, MAST, the software-RCA scoping). Gaps: (i) the survey base for "LLM/multi-agent FDD evidence remains scarce" is a single 2026 *Processes* review — add at least one more 2024–2025 survey or empirical LLM-FDD comparison, plus one PSE-community perspective on foundation models (the auditability objection IDD answers has been raised there); (ii) no RAG-augmented FDD system is cited despite RAG being an IDD layer — the only citation is generic (lewis2020rag); add one domain RAG-FDD work. Also: Table 1 and §2.2 lean on AgentRCA's 40.0%/61.5% figures from an arXiv preprint (2607.22385) I cannot independently verify — double-check the identifier and quote page/section in the supplement, as was done for FE (whose transcription note, `supplementary_fe_table1_transcription.md`, is exemplary and should be cross-referenced explicitly from Table A1's caption).

## Minor Issues

1. **"XMV_14" does not exist — should be XMV_10** (line ~563, §7.3): "the implicated valve XMV_14 itself does not appear in the top-3." TEP has twelve manipulated variables (XMV1–XMV12); the IDV14 actuator is XMV_10, as the paper itself correctly states in §6.4 and Table A2. High-priority typo: it sits in the paper's best baseline-contrast paragraph.
2. **Abstract readability.** The abstract carries six or more parenthetical qualification registers (upper-bound/identity-strict/family-level/branch-localized, blind-control counts, determinism scope). Honest, but the contribution is buried. Move the readings taxonomy to a two-line pointer ("three disclosed readings of the scoring instrument; Table 3") and cut roughly a third.
3. **§6.1 IndPenSim sentence** ("…is the benchmark case definition's, not the dataset's and not this paper's diagnosis---and a case definition that may itself under-describe the batch disturbance---with one normal batch as control; …") is a single ~90-word nested qualification. Split into three sentences: truth channel; case-definition deviation description and its provenance; the disclosure that it may under-describe the batch.
4. **"deliberately weak signature"** for IDV3 (§6.3): the simulator is not deliberate; use "documented weak-signature fault."
5. **Bibliography hygiene**: `yoon2001statistical` key says 2001 but the entry is year 2000 (AIChE J 46 — correct as 2000; rename key or readers of the source will trip); truncated author lists ("and others") in `glm2024chatglm`, `cemri2025mast`, `constantinides2025failuresensoriq`, `chen2023automatic` — expand or use the standard `others` form consistently per Elsevier style; `chen2023automatic` has a published version beyond the arXiv preprint by now.
6. **Table 2 "Consumes: RAG digest"** misleads in isolation: the RAG probe failed in all thirteen runs and ontologies were built from the documented fallback (§3.2). Add a table footnote.
7. **Table 5 caption**: state that the FE-protocol replication's 6/6 is on the all-PCA-detectable six-fault subset (the easiest regime for candidate-in-prompt scoring) to prevent over-reading against FE's 7/11 over eleven faults; and add the baseline-answer selection rule (e.g., "one live run per arm, archived verbatim before any scoring; no re-rolls") — with recorded replays this is the one provenance line still missing from the paper itself.
8. **"one of three blind"** for the controls is repeated at least six times. State once (first use + Table 3 footnote) and cross-refer thereafter.
9. **Figure captions are self-contained mini-articles** (Fig. 3, 5, 6 captions exceed 150 words). AEI copy-editing will push back; move protocol content to the text and keep captions to what the figure shows.
10. **Wilson CIs on purposive scenarios**: correctly computed (I verified all of them), but interpretively strained on n = 9 non-exchangeable scenarios. The caption already calls them "second-order"; better to drop CIs from the headline rows or box the caveat into the table header so the interval is never read as sampling generalization.
11. **Released keyword table**: the per-scenario grading keyword sets (with per-token provenance: documented cause table vs. synonym calibration) are referenced but never tabulated. Add a supplement table — the leave-one-token-out reading ("header" single-token dependency for IDV7) is only reconstructable with it.
12. **Bare-arm coverage gap** (TEP control not run bare) is properly marked "—" in Table A2/Fig. 6, but §7.3 should state the cost in one clause: the bare arm's false-alarm behavior on TEP is unverified (2/2 covered controls only).

## Verdict: MINOR REVISION

**Justification.** This is an unusually self-auditing manuscript, and the self-auditing is load-bearing rather than decorative: the same-model ablation is reported against the paper's own interest (9/9 vs 4/9), the identifier channel is disclosed down to a joint 0/9 floor, the FE transcription carries a verified provenance note, the fabrication incident in the framework's own history is reported in §5 (C4), and every numeric claim I recomputed (Wilson intervals, rubric means, counterfactual commitment counts, hydraulic ratios) checks out. The per-mechanism statistical signatures are the strongest domain content I have evaluated in this area recently — the IDV11 frozen-mean variance burst, the IDV14 stiction limit cycle with quantised stepping against the Horch/Hägglund/Choudhury literature, the IDV7 constant-flow/gain-loss hydraulic argument, and the SKAB cavitation cap naming valve-position and high-frequency suction pressure are all technically correct and correctly cited, and the within-family form discrimination (step/random/stiction on one physical channel) is a genuine evidential demonstration that label recall alone cannot produce. The deployment section is realistic to an expert eye — the swinging-door compression warning is exactly the failure mode practitioners hit when exporting historian data, and the IEC 61511 / GxP boundaries are drawn with appropriate hedging.

Why not accept: (i) the resolved-verdict evidence remains identifier-contaminated with the remedy merely committed (M1) — since it costs ≈16 h of the authors' own pipeline, it should be in the revision; (ii) the process-quality claim is carried by author-designed, ceiling-limited instruments with expert anchoring outstanding (M2); (iii) the digest-primed baseline naming needs precision (M3); and (iv) the Register-A comparison needs recording-provenance pinned (M4), plus M5–M6 and the dozen minor items, one of which (XMV_14) is a factual slip. None of these requires new methodology or threatens the paper's architecture; all are bounded, concrete fixes. Rejecting or demanding major revision would hold the paper to a standard its own honesty has already half-met; accepting without the M1/M2 fixes would let the two remaining evidential gaps persist past the point where the paper itself calls them outstanding.
