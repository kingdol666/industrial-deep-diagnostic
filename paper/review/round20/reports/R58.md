## Assessment

The system description is, with two exceptions below, unusually faithful to the released
code and event logs: counts, file names, checkpoint predicates, error codes, reason
strings, and per-run degradation records all verify exactly, and the §5 execution-form
disclosure (inline execution, marker census, verbatim reason quote) checks out against the
canonical logs. The two exceptions are both one-clause fixes: the §4.1 cap-attribution
parenthetical is factually wrong about the 0.70 cap's provenance, and the declared-parallel
5a/5b pair is never disclosed as having executed serially in the twelve graded runs, despite
the paper's own exactness standard and the logs' explicit "serialized with 5b" record.

## Verdict

MINOR_REVISION. The architecture and execution-form narrative is verified true of the
codebase and canonical runs in 10 of 12 checks (several verbatim); the two mismatches are
localized, easily fixed, and do not undermine any headline number.
