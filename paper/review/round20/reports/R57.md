# R57 — AEI Blind Review (Round 20): Empirical Methods & Statistics

Reviewer role: empirical methods and statistics (design defensibility, uncertainty quantification,
baseline fairness, honest disclosure). Blind review: only the paper (main.tex + rendered pages) and
the repository ground truth were consulted; no prior-round reports were read.

---

## 1. Summary of the evaluated claims

The paper reports a 12-scenario benchmark (9 documented faults, 3 controls) of a nine-stage agentic
diagnosis pipeline: resolved Top-1 6/9 (66.7%, Wilson 95% CI [35.4, 87.9]; 5/9 pre-revision), top-k
8/9, TEP subset 5/6 (4/6 pre-revision), controls 3/3 with zero false alarms, rubric mean 97.9,
judge gate mean 93.1, plus model-controlled baselines (bare same-model LLM 9/9), determinism 45/45,
stability 15/15 within-era, and cost 56–220 min/scenario (median ≈109).

## 3. Headline qualifiers and negative-result placement

The 5/9 (and 4/6 TEP) pre-revision qualifiers appear on every surface that states the headline:
abstract (p.1), contribution 4 (§1), §5 sensitivity paragraph, Table 7 caption (p.37), §7.1, §8.1
text, Fig. 6 caption (p.46), Fig. 7 caption (p.47), §9 first paragraph (p.57, "5/9 under the
pre-revision keyword set"), §10 (p.63), and Table A1's caption. The IDV3 outcome is stated as a
mechanism-level miss in the abstract, §7.2 ("we report this as what it is"), Fig. 3(a) (amber
segment), Fig. 8 (amber "unranked"), and Table A1. The bare-LLM 9/9 > 6/9 result is in the abstract,
§8.3, §9, and §10 — the paper repeatedly subordinates its own accuracy to the ablation. Ceiling
compliance is explicitly labelled circular ("a property of the protocol's arithmetic, not an
empirical discovery"), the rubric as author-designed with limited discriminative power, verdict-type
compliance as one-directional, every point estimate as one stochastic draw, and the repair loop as
a selection effect with the counts disclosed. This is, by my measurement, an unusually honest
results section; every negative disclosure I tested traced to a released artifact.

## 5. Issues

### Must-fix
None. I found no claim-vs-reality mismatch in any area I tested, no statistical error, and no
unfair baseline framing (Register A/B separation, no cross-system significance test, and per-fault
descriptive framing are all correct).

### Should-fix
1. **§8.3 (Table 8 narrative), p.52-55.** The FE-protocol replication on the same GLM model scores
   6/6 on the TEP subset in *all three* regimes — strictly above IDD's 5/6 — but the narrative
   foregrounds FE-official's 5/6 as "identical to IDD's TEP accuracy". One sentence explicitly
   acknowledging that the same-model FE-protocol replication also exceeds IDD on the TEP subset
   would make the ablation discussion as symmetric as the bare-LLM disclosure (the data are already
   honestly printed in Table 8; evidence: `baselines.json.summary.fe_protocol_replication_*`).
2. **§5, Table 4 row 1 (p.26).** "Calibrated set applied ... before re-executions" is the only
   chronology row without an independent timestamp; the journal pins the grading-side switch
   (20:06–20:09Z), but the working-tree edit time itself is unstamped. Mark this row as inferred
   (the remaining rows are journal/git-pinned and I verified them).
3. **§8.3, Table 8 IDD row (p.55).** The row repeats "6/9 (3 capped competing_set)" without the
   pre-revision 5/9 qualifier that every other headline surface carries; add it for uniformity.
4. **Fig. 3(b) (p.38).** Y-axis labels are truncated ("Control: IndPenSim batch-1..."); widen the
   label margin or shorten scenario names.
5. **§10 (p.63).** "rubric mean 97.9/100" appears in the conclusion's list of execution proofs;
   given its disclosed conformance-only semantics, gloss it as conformance there (the Table 7
   caption already does) or drop it.
6. **Abstract (p.1).** A single ~380-word block; splitting into two paragraphs (problem+system /
   results+bounds) would help AEI readers. Non-statistical, cosmetic.

## 7. Verdict

MINOR_REVISION. From an empirical-methods standpoint this is a defensible, correctly
uncertainty-quantified, honestly disclosed benchmark paper: all seven recomputed Wilson intervals
are exact, the median with even n is computed correctly, the keyword sensitivity reproduces
independently from released materials, single-execution and power limitations are stated where the
numbers appear, and every negative result I tested (bare-LLM superiority, IDV3 miss, rubric
circularity, control-coverage gap, IDV14 period instability, verdict-type miss) is disclosed rather
than absorbed. The remaining issues are presentational asymmetries (Table 8 narrative, one
un-stamped chronology row, one missing qualifier), not statistical defects.
