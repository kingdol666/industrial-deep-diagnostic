# R12 — Skeptical General Review (Round 8, blind)

Reviewer role: SKEPTICAL GENERAL (fresh, blind to prior rounds). Whole-paper read: promise vs
delivery, coherence, novelty positioning, writing, reproducibility/ethics statements, and
claim-vs-reality verification against the repository. Read-only; no prior reports consulted.

Paper: `paper/main.tex` (72 pp compiled). Verified against repository ground truth
(results/, workspace/diagnostic-runs/, scripts/benchmark/, app/backend/, .claude/skills/,
git history) and the rendered page images `paper/review/round8/pages/p-01..72.png`.

---

## 1. Overall impression

This is an unusually disciplined systems paper: a nine-stage agentic diagnosis pipeline wrapped in
machine-checked gates, evaluated on a 12-scenario benchmark with a model-controlled baseline suite,
and written with a degree of self-limitation that is rare (the bare-LLM 9/9 ablation is stated in
the abstract itself and correctly re-frames every accuracy claim toward auditability rather than
accuracy). The prose is dense but controlled; the four numbering schemes are explicitly declared
independent; the negatives (three capped COMPETING_SET misses, the single verdict-type compliance
miss, the outstanding retest contract, the author-designed rubric/keywords, the single model
family) are disclosed prominently rather than buried.

I verified 14 claim areas against the repository. Thirteen match exactly. One checkable claim —
the keyword-sensitivity re-scoring — is **contradicted by the released history** when the grader's
own matching logic is re-applied to the pre-revision keyword sets. Because that claim is the
robustness argument for the paper's headline Top-1 number, it must be corrected before publication.

## 3. Abstract, contributions, conclusion — promise vs delivery

- Abstract promises: 9-stage orchestration, L1–L7 grading, four-condition anti-spurious filter,
  machine-checked evaluation — all delivered and verified. "IDD resolves 6/9 … ranks the true
  mechanism among the candidates in two more … three scenarios … capped COMPETING_SET … no control
  raises a false alarm" — all match the released gradings. "On the TEP subset IDD is Top-1 correct
  on 5/6 faults without candidate lists" — matches. The bare-LLM 9/9 sentence is the abstract's
  best feature: it honestly bounds the accuracy claim ("its demonstrated margins … are auditability,
  calibrated uncertainty, and gated process guarantees rather than accuracy") and the conclusion
  repeats the bound. No overclaim detected in the abstract or conclusion.
- Contribution 2's "In the corpus none of the determined verdicts is wrong" — verified
  (determined-verdict precision 6/6; overconfident = 0 in metrics.json).
- Contribution 3's integrity-audit narrative (scripted shortcut found, deleted, benchmark
  re-executed) is consistent with the released audit note (`docs/benchmark/AUDIT-2026-09-13.md`
  referenced from Sec. 8.4) and with the grader's completeness gate (Table 4); I could not
  re-adjudicate the history, but the disclosure itself is prominent and falsifiable.
- Contribution 4's per-fault claims (matches o1-preview 4/4 on shared detectable faults; converts
  GPT-4o's IDV1 miss; scores the PCA-excluded pair) — internally consistent with Table A1 and
  Fig. 6, and explicitly read "descriptively, not as a superiority claim" with the model confound
  flagged. Correctly bounded.

## 4. Novelty positioning

Well done. FaultExplainer is positioned precisely (two prompt regimes, alias acceptance, the four
PCA-excluded faults, and the honest note that FE's own text reports 8/11 while naming two failing
faults — transcribed, not silently copied). AgentRCA (2026), Gong et al., CausalKGPT, FD-LLM and
other AEI-adjacent work are covered; Table 1's four-element gap (real series + mechanism-level RCA
with evidence trails + calibrated three-state verdicts + machine-verifiable evaluation contract)
is defensible, and Sec. 2.4 honestly assigns the intellectual ancestry (Parasuraman, Lee & See,
Heuer, Hill, Chow, OntoCAPE) — "a domain-specific, machine-checked instantiation … not their
invention". This is the right novelty register for AEI.

## 5. Structure, writing, figures

- Four numbering schemes: declared independent and never conflated in the text; Step 3.5 =
  data-processor Phase 5.5 is explained twice and used consistently. One wrinkle: **CP-9 is
  attached to Step 8.5 (HTML review gate) in Table 3 and Algorithm 1, but to Step 9
  (evidence-closure finalisation) in Sec. 3.3's body text and in the Fig. 2 node
  ("CP-9 · overall PASS")**. Minor but real; unify.
- Terminology is defined before use (the three protocol states get an explicit "carry no everyday
  meaning" paragraph; DET/CS/ND legends appear in the tables that need them). Notation in Eqs. (1)–(2)
  is consistent and matches the released confidence.json semantics.
- Figures reviewed as rendered images: Fig. 1 (five-plane architecture, p.13), Fig. 2 (pipeline
  flow, p.14), Fig. 3 (benchmark outcomes, p.35), Fig. 4 (confidence chart, p.39), Fig. 5 (case
  study, p.41), Fig. 6 (per-fault TEP matrix, p.44), Fig. 7 (forest, p.45), Fig. 8 (suite matrix,
  p.50) are all legible, print-quality, colour-blind-safe, and every number I cross-checked
  matches the released artifacts. Two blemishes: Fig. 4 and Fig. 5 sit on float pages with a
  large blank top half (pp. 39, 41); and Fig. 5's chain strip begins "L1 raw record", which
  collides with the paper's careful "no L1 direct measurement" framing for this very scenario
  (L1 there denotes the raw recording, but the juxtaposition invites misreading).
- Length: 72 pp in elsarticle preprint format; the main text closes around p. 57 and the appendix
  carries the machine-readable tables. Long for AEI but not bloated; acceptable.

## 6. Reproducibility and ethics posture

Strong. Data-availability statement enumerates the released evaluation repository (and I confirmed
its index against disk, check 10); a Zenodo DOI is promised on acceptance (acceptable, not yet
minted). CRediT, funding, competing-interest, and GenAI declarations are all present; the GenAI
statement correctly separates writing assistance from the system under study. Negatives are
prominent: the three capped misses, the single compliance miss, the bare-LLM 9/9 bound, the
single-execution/single-model caveats, the outstanding retest contract, the author-designed
instruments, and the leakage-resistant but non-random scenario selection. The one posture failure
is the keyword-sensitivity claim (check 12): a robustness assertion that the released history
contradicts, with no released artifact behind it. Fix the claim and release the re-scoring script;
the timing disclosure itself is accurate.

   `zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs:373-390`; `git 221d061^` keywords; gradings/tep_d01 kw_hits=['配比']).
   Rewrite: state that the calibration moved tep_d01 from miss to hit (Top-1 5/9 → 6/9), that top-k
   is 8/9 under both sets with a different miss, and release the re-scoring script with the
   repository.
2. Anchor the corrected sensitivity statement in the abstract-adjacent claims chain: Sec. 9
   (Discussion) and the conclusion should not imply keyword-robustness of the 6/9 headline until
   item 1 is fixed (currently they do not repeat the exact-reproduction claim, so this is a
   consistency pass after item 1 lands).

## 9. Verdict

MINOR_REVISION. The paper's descriptions match the released system at a level I rarely see
(13 of 14 verification areas exact, including every headline number, every protocol constant, the
case-study numerics, the architecture claims, and the negative disclosures). The single blocking
defect is the keyword-sensitivity exact-reproduction claim, which my re-execution of the grader's
own logic refutes (5/9 under pre-revision keywords) and which must be corrected and re-released;
with that one-sentence-class correction plus the small consistency fixes, the paper is publishable
in AEI.
