# R9 — Empirical Methods & Statistics Review (Round 8, blind)

Reviewer focus: §1 claims vs delivery; §4 formalization (min-form ceiling, elimination contract,
five-factor weights); §5 C1–C5 (timing disclosure, keyword-sensitivity claim); §7–§8 (stability,
determinism, FE fairness, ablation honesty). All Wilson intervals recomputed independently; the
keyword-sensitivity claim was **recomputed from the frozen corpus with the grader's own matching
logic**. Ground truth read only from the repository (paper + released artifacts + git); no prior
review material consulted.

---

## 1. Overall assessment

The empirical discipline of this paper is, with one important exception, exemplary. Every headline
number I checked traces to a released machine-readable artifact and reproduces exactly: metrics,
rubric deductions, judge scores, per-scenario confidences, stability pair counts, baseline arm
results, PCA detection rates, determinism verdict, case-study quantities, repair counts, and the
corpus-wide RAG claim. All seven Wilson 95% intervals in Tables 2/6/A1 and Fig. 7 are correct to
the printed precision (recomputed by hand). The ablation honesty is outstanding: the paper's own
model-controlled evidence (bare same-model call 9/9 vs pipeline 6/9) is stated in the abstract and
used to bound the accuracy claim, rather than buried.

The exception is material. The paper's keyword-sensitivity sentence in §5 (C1) — that re-scoring
the frozen corpus under the pre-revision keyword sets "reproduces the headline outcomes exactly
(Top-1 6/9, top-k 8/9 under both sets)" — is **factually wrong**. I recomputed it. Under the
pre-revision keyword sets (git `221d061^`), applied with the grader's exact text-building and
substring logic (`scripts/benchmark/zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs`, ~lines 366–390), the frozen corpus
scores **Top-1 = 5/9, not 6/9**: tep_d01 loses its hit entirely, because its only keyword hit is
the calibrated synonym `配比` (the released grading record itself records `kw_hits: ["配比"]`),
and **none** of the six pre-revision tep_d01 keywords (`a/c feed`, `feed ratio`, `进料比`,
`stream 4`, `a and c`, `成分`) occurs anywhere in any of the four tep_d01 diagnosis artifacts.
Top-k remains 8/9 in count, but the membership changes (under the old set tep_d03 hits via the
removed generic token `温度` and tep_d01 misses; under the new set the reverse) — so "exactly" is
wrong on both counts. One of the paper's six Top-1 successes is therefore an artifact of the
calibration. The related timing sentence ("the calibration was applied once, uniformly, *before
any grading was computed*") also overstates what the revision note and journal support.

Because the paper's central thesis is auditability, these are blocking errors — but they are
textual corrections plus one disclosure, not a call for new experiments. **Verdict: minor
revision**, conditional on the must-fix list below.

### 2.3 §5 C1–C5 and the keyword-sensitivity recompute

Timing: the run orderings the paper states do hold (canonical runs 2026-09-15 04:53–04:54 +0800 <

revision note itself claims only "修订先于 v2 最终聚合一次性uniform应用" (applied uniformly before
the v2 final aggregation) — narrower than "before any grading was computed". The uniform,
non-per-result application is plausible (the new sets tighten some scenarios and the old sets
remain public), but the paper's sentence must be reworded to match the note, and the
tep_d01 flip should be disclosed.

C2–C5 verified: 153/153 fingerprints claim consistent with the repro gate; 14-artifact set
(Table 4) matches the run directories; C5's "internally consistent, deliberately not an independent

## 5. Verdict

**MINOR_REVISION.** The quantitative skeleton of the paper is reproducible to an unusual degree,
and the ablation/framing is more honest than most LLM-evaluation papers. But the paper cannot
ship with a factually false robustness sentence about its own scoring instrument: the
keyword-sensitivity claim must be corrected (Top-1 is 5/9 under the pre-revision sets; tep_d01's
hit is calibration-dependent; top-k's 8/9 holds only in count with a membership swap), the timing
sentence aligned with the revision note and journal, and the Table 9 run-ID pointer fixed. All
three fixes are textual; the underlying corpus need not change, and the corrected sensitivity
statement is still compatible with the paper's thesis — indeed it strengthens the argument for
treating the keyword sets as author-designed instruments with disclosed failure cases.

PUBLISH-READY: no — one evaluation-integrity claim is contradicted by the released artifacts and
my recomputation, and a paper selling machine-checked auditability must not contain it.
