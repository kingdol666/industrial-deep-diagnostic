**PASS — Fig 3 (p-35), Fig 7 (p-45), Figs 1-2 (TikZ).** Fig 3(a): blue = Top-1 DETERMINED,

**FAIL (blocking) — Fig 4, p-39 (`fig_calibration`).** The five TEP DETERMINED bars are drawn in
**SKY #56B4E9 — the hue that means "capped COMPETING_SET, true mechanism ranked" in Figs 3(a) and
8.** Pixel census of `fig_calibration.png` finds **zero** `#0072B2` (IDD blue) pixels; the only
blues present are sky. Cause is visible in `make_figures.py:182`
(`base = BLUE if skab else SKY if tep else GREEN` — dataset-colouring overrides verdict
colouring; in this corpus the only DETERMINED faults are 5 TEP + 1 IndPenSim). Two consequences:
 (i) convention violation: blue = IDD determined is not honoured anywhere in the figure;
 (ii) **caption-figure colour mismatch**: the caption says "Solid **blue**/green bars are
DETERMINED fault verdicts; hatched sky-blue bars are capped COMPETING_SET…" but the "blue" and
the "sky-blue" it names are the *same drawn colour* (#56B4E9); only the hatch distinguishes
verdict states. A reader cross-referencing Fig 3(a)/Fig 8 will misread the solid sky DET bars.
*Fix (one line): colour DETERMINED bars `#0072B2` (keep green or also normalise IndPenSim to
blue), keep sky+hatch for capped CS; alternatively keep dataset hues and rewrite the caption to
declare them — but the former restores the global convention.*

**FAIL (blocking) — Fig 6, p-44 (`fig_tep`).** IDD Top-1 cells are **dark green `#0a7d38`**
(`make_figures.py:231`), not blue; FE "correct" cells are `#009E73` green. So (i) blue = IDD
determined is violated (blue appears only as the column-divider line); (ii) two similar greens
with *different meanings* (FE-correct vs IDD-correct) sit side by side, distinguished almost
solely by lightness — colourblind-hostile; (iii) green here = "correct", while on the very next
page (Fig 7) green = controls, and in Fig 8 green = resolved/hit/pass. The caption names **no**
colours and there is no in-figure legend, so the reader gets no disambiguation. Cell text
("correct/wrong/Top-1 (0.79)") is self-labelling, which is why this is survivable, but it is the
weakest visual piece of the paper. *Fix: IDD column cells → `#0072B2`; FE correct cells → grey
or a caption-declared hue; add a one-line colour key to the caption. Note the FE "wrong" cell is
vermillion `#D55E00`, correctly distinct from amber — keep that.*

**BORDERLINE — Fig 8, p-50 (`fig_suite_matrix`).** Best legended figure in the paper (5-swatch
legend), sky/amber/vermillion semantics exactly conform. Two reservations: (a) IDD DETERMINED is
green here (caption does declare "green resolved DETERMINED (Top-1)… green 'normal' for
controls", and the legend disambiguates, so caption-figure agreement holds) — but combined with
Fig 6 and Fig 7 the reader meets green as *three* different things in four pages; (b) the caption
claims the PCA rate ramp is "an independent sequential ramp … that **does not reuse the semantic
colours** of columns 1–3", yet the ramp (`make_figures.py:298`) runs white→`#CCE3DC`→`#2E6E63`
— a teal whose hue (~168°) is the same family as semantic green `#009E73` (~165°). At the dark
end (rate 100) the cells read as "another green". Either re-key the ramp to a neutral/grey-purple
sequence or soften the caption sentence. Cosmetic-to-moderate.

**Convention summary:** amber discipline exemplary; sky/blue/green semantics are inconsistent
across Figs 4, 6, 7, 8 (three meanings for green; determined drawn in three different hues:
blue/green/sky). A single "verdict colour = fixed hue" pass through `make_figures.py` fixes all.
