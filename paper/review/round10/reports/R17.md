# R17 — Empirical Methods & Statistics Review (Round 10, blind)

Reviewer focus: defensibility of experimental design, uncertainty quantification, baseline
fairness, and honest disclosure. All claims below were checked against the repository ground
truth; several headline statistics were independently recomputed.

## 0. Verdict summary

The quantitative core of this paper is unusually verifiable and, after extensive recomputation,
every number I checked matched the released artifacts exactly: all seven printed Wilson intervals,
the headline 6/9 / 8/9 / 97.9 / 93.1 aggregates, the keyword-sensitivity delta (5/9 vs 6/9, top-k
8/9 both), the determinism verdict (45/45), the stability decomposition (15/15 = 12 pre-discipline
case-versions + 3 current), the seeded re-test draw, the baseline-suite counts, and the C1
calibration timeline (journal timestamps and commit time). The §4 formalization of the
three-state confidence ceiling is precise and, rare for this kind of paper, correctly separates
agent-documented caps from machine-enforced gates, and agent-applied contract parts from
machine-checked ones. Two qualification/wording defects remain, both in the paper's own currency
(claim qualification), plus a handful of presentation nits. No data-integrity obstacle was found.
