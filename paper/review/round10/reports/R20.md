# R20 — Skeptical General Review (Round 10, blind)

Reviewer role: AEI general reviewer, fresh/blind. Whole-paper read of `paper/main.tex` (72 pp,
review format) plus rendered pages `paper/review/round10/pages/p-01..72.png`. Claims verified
against the repository. Blindness rule respected (nothing under `paper/review/` read except this

---

## 1. Overall impression

This is a systems paper whose central promise is *description-matches-system*: an agentic
industrial RCA pipeline whose headline numbers are deliberately bounded, and whose evaluation
framework is machine-checked. I therefore treated claim-vs-reality fidelity as the primary
criterion and ran 11 spot checks mixing numbers, artifact paths, architecture-vs-code, and
corpus-level probes (Sections 4–5 below). **Ten of eleven checks matched the released
repository exactly; one minor off-by-one factual slip was found.** The abstract, the four
contributions, and the conclusion are, without exception, delivered in the body, and the
paper's self-imposed qualifiers (single execution, wide Wilson intervals, author-designed
instruments, bare-LLM superiority on accuracy, outstanding retest contract) all appear where
they should.

## 3. Novelty, positioning, and reproducibility posture

- **vs FaultExplainer.** §2.2 is the most careful FE discussion I have seen: it separates FE's
  two prompt regimes, states the alias policy, flags FE's internal inconsistency (names faults
  10/13 as failing yet reports 8/11 — transcribed as reported), and derives the three protocol
  properties (top-3+alias vs strict single verdict; PCA-excluded IDV3/4 scored by IDD; alias
  conflates IDV11/IDV14). The Register A/B separation and the refusal to claim significance
  across systems is the right posture, and §8.1 explicitly downgrades the per-fault table to
  descriptive once the 9/9 ablation is known.
- **vs multi-agent LLM RCA.** AgentRCA (July 2026) is engaged with its own numbers (40.0% Top-1,
  61.5% Top-2, ten TEP faults) and its gap (ranked list, no calibrated confidence, no external
  truth harness) is stated. Table 1's positioning axes (scenario-level RCA, evidence trail,
  calibration, repro gate) are defensible; the claim that (iii)/(iv) are absent from every cited
  system survived my reading of the cited works' descriptions in §2.
- **Intellectual ancestry (§2.4)** explicitly disclaims inventing ACH, Hill's criteria, Chow's
  reject option, Parasuraman/Lee&See, OntoCAPE — an unusually honest novelty scoping that
  strengthens rather than weakens the contribution claim.
- **Reproducibility posture.** Strong and verified: released artifact index (Table A2) matches
  disk row-for-row (check 5 below); data-availability statement enumerates what is actually in
  the repo; Zenodo DOI promised only on acceptance (no fake DOI); CRediT (single author),
  competing-interest, funding, and GenAI declarations are present and candid — the GenAI
  statement separates the writing-assistance tools from the IDD system under study, which is the
  correct disclosure for this paper.
- **Prominent negatives** (capped misses, bare-LLM superiority, single-execution evidence,
  IDV14's unstable fine-grained period estimate across sessions, one-directional verdict typing,
  rubric circularity, partially executed ablation matrix, missing expert anchoring) are each
  stated in the body, not buried. This is AEI-grade honesty.

## 6. MUST-FIX (blocking)

1. **§3.1 (p.10), factual count error:** "five of the eighteen packages (the optional layers) do
   not [carry the protocol checklist]" — the released tree shows **six** of 18 packages without
   `references/agent-protocol.md` (darwin-skill, diagnostic-html-visualizer, industrial-
   analysis-auto, industrial-analysis-enhance-auto, industrial-data-preprocessor,
   rag-knowledge-builder). One word, but it is a checkable claim about the released system in a
   paper whose thesis is checkable claims. (The companion claim "all eight dispatched packages
   carry the checklist" is correct.)
2. **Abstract length:** ~430 words against Elsevier/AEI's ~250-word guidance; the qualifier
   chain ("5/9 under the pre-revision keyword set…") must survive compression, not be cut.

No other blocking issues: I found no unsupported accuracy claim, no missing negative, no
artifact index row absent from disk, and no architecture claim contradicted by code.

## 7. SHOULD-FIX (non-blocking)

1. "15/15 case-version pairs" (§8.4/§9): these are case-versions with ≥2 recorded runs, not
   pairwise counts (IndPenSim batch093 v1 has 3 runs = 3 pairs counted as 1); define the unit once.
2. Consolidate the capped-verdict narrative and the RAG-status paragraph; reclaim 2–3 pages.
3. Timezone: the paper cites local timestamps (04:06–04:09, 04:53–04:54) while the released
   `journal.jsonl` stores UTC (20:06Z); add "(UTC+8)" once so readers can audit the journal.
4. Repair-count disclosure ("eleven scenarios zero repairs, one control one round") is only
   indirectly checkable in event logs (batch001: "judge executed inline with 2 iterations");
   release a one-line per-scenario repair-count table to make it directly checkable.
5. Fig. 3(b) scenario labels are at the legibility edge at print size; Fig. 2's float page
   strands half of p.15.
6. §3.1's claude/omp engines are special-cased outside HARNESS_DEFS (12 entries) in
   `engines.mjs`; a half-sentence footnote would preempt pedantic code readers.

## 8. Verdict

The paper does the rare thing it promises: every headline number I checked recomputes from the
released artifacts, every promised negative is prominent, and the one discrepancy found is a
single-word count. Writing, positioning, and figure quality are at or above AEI standard; the
statistical power is inherently limited but is disclosed to a fault. With the count fixed and
the abstract compressed, this is publishable.

**Recommendation: MINOR REVISION (favorable).**
