# R26 — Industrial-Informatics Systems Review (Round 12, blind)

Reviewer role: AEI industrial-informatics systems reviewer. Mandate: every statement the paper
makes about the system must be TRUE of the real codebase; architecture, modules, and
collaboration must be unmistakable. Blindness rule respected (no files under `paper/review/`
other than the briefing and this report were read).

Materials reviewed: `paper/main.tex` (full source, 74 pp build), rendered pages
`paper/review/round12/pages/p-04.png` … `p-18.png` for Sections 2–3 and the appendix start,
and the repository ground truth. Focus: §2–§3 architecture claims. I performed 12
claim-vs-reality spot checks (requirement was ≥5).

---

## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually verifiable systems paper. Nearly every architectural statement in §2–§3 —
package census, dispatch topology, engine-layer realization, checkpoint predicates, repair
governance, numbering schemes, RAG per-run variance, ports and error codes — maps one-to-one
onto an artifact I could open and count. I found **no false architecture claim**. Two sentences
are stronger than the evidence (Table 2 caption "read and write only these files"; the RAG
"fallbacks recorded in the event logs" clause covering four runs whose logs record a store-reuse
fast path, not a fallback event). Both are one-line wording fixes. Layout has avoidable
whitespace on pp. 16–18 from float repacking.

The paper is also candid where it matters: the bare-LLM 9/9 vs pipeline 6/9 ablation is stated
in the abstract, the rubric is declared author-designed with limited discriminative power, and
the single-execution caveat is repeated at every use.

## 4. Verdict rationale

All twelve of my spot checks returned match (two with wording-level nuances). The two blocking
items are one-sentence fixes: soften the Table 2 caption's "only these files" claim, and correct
the RAG parenthetical so that "fallbacks recorded in the event logs" is claimed only for the runs
whose logs actually record RAG-fallback events (tep_d00, tep_d01, batch001, batch093) while
tep_d03/d04/d07/d11 are described as store-reuse fast-path bypasses (which is what their logs
record). With those two edits and the float tightening, this is publishable.
