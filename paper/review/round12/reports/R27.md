## 1. Colour-convention audit, end to end

Convention under test: IDD dark blue = DETERMINED in every result figure; sky+hatch = capped CS ranked;
amber (one hue, dark text) = capped CS unranked; green = FE hits/control cells; grey = FE/unscored.

Verified in the drawn figures (not just the script):

| Convention element | Fig 3a | Fig 3b | Fig 4 | Fig 5 | Fig 6 | Fig 7 | Fig 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IDD dark blue #0072B2 = DETERMINED | yes (bars) | yes (fault markers) | yes (DET bars; **batch-93 correctly unified blue, no green DET bar remains**) | signal trace only | yes (IDD column) | yes (IDD rows) | yes (Top-1 cells) |
| sky #56B4E9 + black `///` hatch = capped CS ranked | **yes, hatched** (SKAB bar) | n/a | **yes, hatched** (valve 0.65, cav 0.58) | n/a | n/a (no CS ranked in TEP subset) | n/a | **yes, hatched** (2 cells) |
| amber #E69F00 = capped CS unranked | yes (TEP miss segment, solid) | n/a | yes (IDV3 0.60 solid) | pale-amber *shading* (different, lighter — no collision) | yes (IDV3 cell, text #3a2600) | n/a | yes (IDV3 cell, text #3a2600) |
| green #009E73 = FE hit / control | n/a | n/a | controls are grey (correct — green reserved) | pale-green band (shading, distinct) | yes (FE-correct) | yes (controls 3/3) | yes (hit/normal + col-6 det.) |
| grey | n/a | controls | hatched grey controls | n/a | pale grey unscored/yes; grey "no" | FE baselines | n/a / scope cells |

The hatch placement matches the stated target set exactly: **Figs 3a, 4, 8** carry the black `///` hatch on
sky cells/bars; Figs 5/6/7 draw no hatch and their captions claim none. Amber is one constant hue
(`ORANGE = #E69F00`) in all four figures where it appears, always with dark (near-black/brown) text.

Grayscale verification (figures converted to L): the hatch does real work — sky (lum 157.9) vs amber
(162.1) differ by ~1.6% in luminance, so without the hatch the ranked/unranked CS split would be
invisible in grayscale; with the hatch, all four Fig 4 categories (solid dark, hatched sky, solid amber,
hatched pale grey) and all four Fig 8 column-1 states are unambiguous in grayscale. This is a correct,
well-engineered encoding. Residual grayscale notes: in Fig 6 green (105.9) vs vermillion (118.9) cells
are near-identical grey — acceptable because the cell text ("correct"/"wrong") carries the semantics; in
Fig 8 the same pair is likewise text-backed. The Fig 8 PCA ramp is teal, saturating to a greenish dark
end that is visually distinct from (but hue-neighbour to) the FE green — acceptable, side-by-side
distinct; the caption's "does not reuse the semantic colours" is defensible.

## 2. Caption-vs-figure agreement (the required check)

- **Fig 3 (p36) — FAIL (blocking, one-word fix).** The drawn SKAB bar in (a) carries the black `///`
  hatch (clearly visible in the render and in `make_figures.py` `hatch="///"`), but the caption says only
  "(sky blue)" — **the hatch state is not named**, while the Fig 4 caption says "hatched sky-blue bars"
  and the Fig 8 caption says "hatched sky". This is exactly the caption-figure disagreement the paper's
  own convention forbids, and it breaks the grayscale reading path (a grayscale reader is told "sky blue"
  — which is indistinguishable from amber — with no hint that the pattern, not the tone, is the signal).
  Fix: "capped COMPETING_SET verdicts whose survivors rank the true mechanism (hatched sky blue)".

- **Fig 7 (p46) — PASS.** Grey / Blue / Green named and drawn as described; all six printed estimates and
  CIs match the text exactly (see §4 below).
- **Fig 8 (p55) — MOSTLY PASS.** Column 1 is fully specified including hatch ("hatched sky capped
  COMPETING_SET with the true mechanism still ranked"), the ramp is specified, the grey "no" control is
