# R28 — Skeptical General Review (Round 12, blind)

Reviewer role: fresh, blind general review for Advanced Engineering Informatics. Whole-paper read
(tex source + rendered pages), with claim-vs-reality verification against the released repository.
Blindness rule respected: nothing under `paper/review/` read except the briefing and `pages/`.

---

## 1. Summary of the paper

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage multi-agent LLM diagnosis pipeline (ontology, deterministic
statistics, competing-hypothesis reasoning, physical verification, judge/auditor gates with bounded
repair) over sensor/process data, with evidence levels L1–L7, a four-condition anti-spurious filter,
and a three-state verdict protocol (DETERMINED / COMPETING_SET / NEEDS_DATA). It contributes a
machine-checked evaluation framework (truth isolation, SHA-256 fingerprinting, stage-authored
provenance-labelled artifacts, 14-artifact completeness gate, artifact-consistency gate) and executes
a 12-scenario benchmark (SKAB 2+1, TEP 6+1, IndPenSim 1+1). Headline: Top-1 6/9 (Wilson CI
[35.4, 87.9]; 5/9 pre-revision keywords), top-k 8/9, TEP subset 5/6 (4/6 pre-revision), controls 3/3,
rubric mean 97.9, plus model-controlled baselines (classical PCA, FE-protocol replication, bare-LLM)
in which the bare same-model call scores 9/9 — *ahead* of the pipeline — bounding every accuracy
claim to process quality rather than accuracy.

## 2. Abstract audit

- Length ~244 words — within the 250-word limit.
- Every promise is delivered in the body, and the sensitive qualifiers are unspun: 6/9 appears with
  its CI *and* the 5/9 pre-revision figure in the abstract itself (§5–6, Table 2, Fig 7 and the
  conclusion repeat both); 5/6 TEP is likewise qualified with 4/6. The abstract explicitly states the
  bare-LLM 9/9 result "—more than the pipeline—", the three capped COMPETING_SET verdicts, and zero
  false alarms. No promise in the abstract failed verification.

## 3. Contributions vs delivery

1. *Quality-gated agentic pipeline* — delivered and verified against code (checkpoints table matches
   the finaliser/rubric implementations; CP-1..CP-9 predicates are mechanical as claimed).
2. *Evidence grading / calibration* — delivered; the L1–L7 arithmetic, five-factor weights
   (25/25/20/20/10) and cap stack (0.70/0.65/0.50 agent-side; 0.90/0.70 machine-side) all match the
   enforcing scripts and the released confidence.json files.
3. *Reproducible evaluation framework* — delivered; C1–C5 are implemented in
   `scripts/benchmark/` and `results/benchmark/` as claimed, including the disclosed fabrication-audit
   history (`docs/benchmark/AUDIT-2026-09-13.md` exists).
4. *12-scenario benchmark with per-fault FE baselines* — delivered; composition matches Table 5 and
   the scenario definitions.

The paper is candid that the contribution is a machine-checked *instantiation* of known frameworks
(Heuer ACH, Hill's criteria, Chow's reject option, Parasuraman/Lee&See, OntoCAPE), "not their
invention" — an unusual and welcome framing that pre-empts the obvious novelty attack.

## 4. Novelty assessment

- vs **FaultExplainer**: the comparison is careful to the point of self-abnegation: candidate lists
  vs none, top-3-with-alias vs strict single verdict, 11-fault detectable set vs 6-fault subset,
  different base models — all stated wherever Register A is invoked, no significance test claimed,
  and the descriptive per-fault table is transcribed with an explicit caveat where FE's own text is
  internally inconsistent (8/11 vs named failures). The FE-official-code replication (5/6, same
  model) is a genuine addition most comparison papers omit.
- vs **multi-agent-LLM RCA / AgentRCA (2026)**: engaged directly with positioning on calibrated
  confidence and the external truth-comparison harness; Table 1's five-column scorecard is
  author-scored for competitors (mildly self-serving per cell, but each cell is justified in text and
  elements (iii)/(iv) are defensibly absent from the cited systems).
- The genuinely new material is the machine-checked evaluation contract (C1–C5), the reason-coded
  per-run ceilings, and the corpus-level provenance discipline — plus the negative-result framing
  (bare LLM wins on accuracy). Enough novelty for AEI as an applied systems paper.

## 6. Reproducibility and ethics posture

- Data availability (supplementary repository + Zenodo DOI on acceptance), CRediT, COI, funding and
  GenAI declarations are all present and appropriate; the GenAI declaration correctly separates the
  writing-assistance tools from the IDD system under study.
- The reproducibility posture is unusually strong: released gradings with provenance labels
  (`judge-agent-10-criteria`, `auditor-agent`, `html-reviewer-agent` — verified in journal records),
  journal-timestamped keyword switch, pre-revision keyword sets recoverable from git (221d061~1),
  released sensitivity counterfactual, seeded retest draws replayable from released seeds, and a
  disclosed integrity audit whose shortcut is now structurally unreplayable by the 14-artifact gate.
- Honest negatives are prominent and concrete: bare-LLM 9/9 > pipeline 6/9 (abstract, §8.3,
  conclusion); covered controls 2/3 with the TEP-control n/a marked as an execution-coverage gap in
  Fig 8; the single verdict-type compliance miss (SKAB valve, R4 −15) narrated as a miss, not a
  success; IDV14's unstable fine-grained period estimate (≈12 vs ≈30 min); the outstanding seeded
  retest contract; ceiling-compliance circularity and the rubric's limited discriminative power both
  stated as such.

## 7. Structure, writing, figures

- The four numbering schemes are explicitly enumerated and de-confounded (§3.3, p.14). Notation
  (Eq. 1–2, Algorithm 1) is defined and used consistently; the small-capped verdict states are
  explicitly de-everydayed.
- Figures as rendered: Fig 1/2 (TikZ) clean and legible; Fig 3, 4, 6, 7 legible with values matching
  Tables 2/3/8; Fig 8 legible and matches baselines.json. Okabe-Ito palette is consistent and
  colour-blind safe.
- Weaknesses:
  (a) §5's C1 paragraph is a single ~450-word paragraph with nested parenthetical timelines — it is
  the paper's most important provenance disclosure and also its hardest-to-read passage.
  (b) Fig 5's in-panel annotation ("lowest quantisation bin (30 L/min): 125 rows, 106 runs") is not
  reconciled with the caption's 39.4% floor dwell (0.394 × the 289-row window ≈ 114 rows); Table 3
  also names the floor bin 30.62 L/min while the figure panel says "30 L/min".
  (c) Page 54 carries five lines and Fig 8 lands on p.55 after Section 8's text has ended — float
  drift the preamble itself disclaims as "best effort", but visible in this build.
  (d) Length: 74 pp in review format (double-spaced preprint); substantial but defensible; ~1/3 is
  tables, figures, protocol and appendix.

## 8. Decision

All fifteen substantive spot checks matched the released repository exactly — an unusual degree of
claim-to-artifact fidelity, including for the paper's self-incriminating numbers. The remaining
issues are presentational and precision-level, plus the inherent (disclosed) limitation that the
grading instruments are author-designed and expert anchoring is outstanding. These warrant a minor
revision, not a rejection or major overhaul.
