# R64 — Sceptical General Review (Round 21, blind)

Reviewer role: fresh, whole-paper read (abstract promise vs delivery, coherence, novelty, writing,
reproducibility/ethics), with claim-vs-reality verification against the released repository.
I read the full TeX source, the rendered pages for the abstract/figures/key tables, and verified
claims against the ground-truth files. I did not read any other file under `paper/review/`.

---

## 1. Overall assessment

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage multi-agent diagnosis pipeline with evidence grading and a
machine-checked benchmark, and reports an unusually sober set of results: 6/9 resolved Top-1
(5/9 pre-revision keywords), an IDV3 mechanism-level miss stated in the abstract, three capped
competing-set verdicts, zero false alarms, and — most unusually — a model-controlled ablation
showing a bare call to the same model resolves 9/9, i.e. the pipeline *loses* on accuracy and its
claimed margins are auditability, calibrated uncertainty and gated process guarantees. The paper's
central promise is that its description matches what the system actually does, and that the
evaluation is "machine-verified against the released execution proofs".

I verified this promise hard. Across twelve targeted claim-vs-reality checks (numbers, artifact
paths, chronology, architecture-vs-code), **eleven verified exactly**, including several claims I
expected to be rhetorical but that are literally true of the released tree (the 14-artifact
completeness list, the keyword-sensitivity flips, the seeded re-test draw, the single RAG success).
**One factual error found**: the C1 chronology table's "Canonical executions" row is shifted by
eight hours (details in Spot-check 6). It does not flip any conclusion — keywords are grading-side
only, so execution timing is informational — but in a table whose selling point is machine-verified
provenance, it must be corrected.

The paper is publishable in AEI after a minor revision.

## 2. Abstract

- 248 words (limit 250) — compliant.
- Promise vs delivery: every abstract claim I traced is delivered in the body and verified on disk
  (6/9 + CI, 5/9 pre-revision, IDV3 miss, 5/6 TEP with 4/6 pre-revision, 9/9 bare-LLM bound,
  capped verdicts, zero false alarms).
- "Internal consistency is machine-verified against the released execution proofs" is carefully
  scoped: Section 5 (C5) states plainly that the gate verifies internal consistency and is
  "deliberately not an independent re-execution of the reasoning layer". This is honest, and the
  conclusion repeats the bounding. No overclaim.

## 3. Contributions vs delivery

1. *Quality-gated agentic pipeline* — delivered; checkpoints CP-1..CP-9 have mechanical predicates
   (Table 2) and the released grader enforces the 14-artifact set (verified against
   `scripts/benchmark/zcode_direct_pipeline.mjs` `PIPELINE_ARTIFACTS`, ~line 288, including the
   <5120 B HTML re-check quoted verbatim in the paper).
2. *Evidence grading and calibration* — delivered; ceiling chain verified end-to-end (Spot-check 5),
   including the reason-coded `DIRECT_MEASUREMENT` 0.75 ceiling on IDV4 that I half-expected to be
   narrative decoration but found verbatim in the released `confidence.json`.
3. *Reproducible evaluation framework* — delivered; truth isolation, 153/153 fingerprints
   (`dataset_manifest.json`; `repro_report.json`: 153 verified / 0 mismatched, 12/12 coverage),
   provenance labels in every grading record, the disclosed integrity-audit history, and the C5
   scope statement.
4. *12-scenario benchmark with per-fault baselines* — delivered; per-fault FE transcription with
   three stated protocol caveats, plus the self-condemning ablation.

The contributions as stated are the contributions as delivered. The prominent negatives (bare-LLM
9/9 in the abstract; the single verdict-type compliance miss; the one-directional typing; the
author-designed rubric; the outstanding expert anchoring) are all stated in the abstract, results
and discussion, not buried.

## 4. Novelty

Against FaultExplainer: the comparison is per-fault at scenario granularity, with the protocol
differences (candidate list, alias acceptance vs strict single verdict, PCA-excluded pair) stated at
every invocation, and the o1-preview/GPT-4o numbers transcribed with the FE-internal inconsistency
(8/11 vs named failures) disclosed. Against AgentRCA and the multi-agent LLM RCA literature: the
positioning (real time series + mechanism-level RCA + calibrated three-state verdicts + machine
verifiable contract) is defensible, and Table 1's five-column positioning is argued, though
self-scored — acceptable given the disclosed caveats. Section 2.4 correctly assigns the intellectual
ancestry (Heuer, Hill, Chow, Parasuraman, Lee & See, OntoCAPE) rather than claiming invention. The
honest-negative ablation is, in my experience, the paper's most distinctive contribution.

## 5. Reproducibility and ethics posture

- Artifact index (Table A2) matches the released tree (briefs, gradings, run directories,
  `benchmark_report_en.md` all present at the stated paths).
- Execution form is disclosed prominently and verified: inline stage execution, 11/12 event logs
  carry explicit markers under varying keys (Spot-check 9), 5a∥5b serialization confirmed in event
  ordering, corollary about shared model sessions stated.
- Cost disclosed three times (56–220 min, median ≈109 — verified exactly, Spot-check 7).
- Data availability, CRediT, COI, funding, and GenAI declarations all present; the GenAI statement

- Remaining gaps are stated as gaps: single execution per scenario, single model family, no expert
  anchoring, engine-substitution and filter-disabled ablation arms unexecuted, repeat study
  pre-registered but partial (with the insufficient-runs disclosure on the drawn scenario).

## 10. Verdict

MINOR_REVISION. The paper's defining risk — that its description does not match what the system
does — is largely refuted by direct verification: headline numbers, ceilings, chronology (except one
row), execution-form disclosures, cost, stability machinery, RAG degradations, baselines and
architecture claims all match the released tree, often verbatim. One factual timebase error in the
C1 table and a caption/colour nit in Fig. 8 are the only defects found; both are one-line fixes. The
honesty posture (bare-LLM 9/9 in the abstract, IDV3 miss unspun, 5/9 and 4/6 pre-revision numbers
repeated everywhere) is exactly what AEI should want, and the remaining limitations are disclosed
rather than discovered.
