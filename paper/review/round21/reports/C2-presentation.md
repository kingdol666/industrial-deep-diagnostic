## Paper Summary

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage agentic diagnosis pipeline (competing-hypothesis reasoning, L1–L7 evidence grading, anti-spurious-correlation filter, gated bounded repair) with a machine-checked 12-scenario benchmark (SKAB, TEP, IndPenSim). Headline: 4/9 faults resolved Top-1 (identity-strict 3/9, joint floor 0/9), top-k 9/9 at family level, zero false alarms, one repeat execution, byte-identical deterministic suite. The presentation load is heavy: 10 figures, 9 main-text tables, 2 appendix tables, 1 algorithm.

## Major Issues

**M1. The abstract is within the word limit but is not self-contained and is over-packed with hedging machinery.**
244 words (limit 250) carrying **9 parentheticals, 6 em-dashes, 4 semicolons**, and at least five instrument terms a first-time reader cannot resolve from the abstract alone: "the keyword instrument's family level", "its stricter branch-localized reading", "identity-strict 3/9", "the joint worst-case floor is 0/9", "one of three blind", "the deterministic suite arms reproduce 45 recorded run outputs byte-identically". Each qualifier is honorably disclosed, but stacked into one paragraph they read as a hedge catalogue and bury the actual contribution (auditable process > accuracy, by the paper's own ablation). Recommendation: keep headline numbers plus at most two caveats in the abstract; move the reading-structure taxonomy (upper-bound / identity-strict / branch-localized / recall-possible) to Section 7.2 where it is defined. This is the single highest-leverage presentation fix in the paper.

**M2. Two appendix tables fall at or below the 6 pt legibility floor.**
Table A2 (`tab:baselinedetail`, p. 59) is `\scriptsize` (7 pt) shrunk by `\resizebox{\textwidth}` at a natural width of ≈16.8 cm against a ≈12.4 cm text block → shrink ≈0.74, effective **≈5.2–5.5 pt** (measured from the render: cap height ≈6.5 px at 110 dpi). Table A1 (p. 58) lands at ≈6.2 pt (normalsize shrunk ≈0.62). Table 9 (p. 46) is borderline at ≈6.3 pt. These are the paper's evidence tables and deserve better. Fix: shorten the enormous captions (see M3), drop the resizebox on A1/A2, split A2 by dataset or set it in a `\small` two-column layout, or rotate. Main-text tables (2, 3, 7 ≈6.2–6.9 pt effective) pass but sit close to the floor; trim their `\tabcolsep` rather than shrinking further.

**M3. Caption discipline: four captions are full paragraphs of analysis that duplicate the body.**
Table 6's caption is ≈230 words (nine sentences of methodological hedging that belongs in Section 7.2/9); Table A1 ≈200 words (the FE no-candidate-regime contradiction discussion is body material, repeated from Section 2.2); Table A2 ≈200 words (a variable-code glossary that should be a footnote or the released README); Table 8 ≈120 words including wall-clock commentary. AEI captions should identify and read the table, not re-argue the paper. Besides wasting ~1.5 pages, the oversized captions are the direct cause of the M2 shrink factors and of the half-empty appendix pages.

## Minor Issues

1. **Keyword block breaks across pages 1→2** ("grading, Process ontology" lands at the top of p. 2). Tighten the frontmatter (the abstract's trailing `\medskip`-scale length is the cause); a keyword list must not split.
2. **Seven keywords.** AEI (Elsevier standard) allows a maximum of six; drop or merge one (e.g., "Evidence grading" into "Fault root-cause analysis").
3. **Caption-vs-image mismatch, Fig. 10:** caption says "*the asterisk marks the brief-blind control*", but in panel (a) the asterisk sits on **"SKAB cav\*"** — the mechanism-blind *fault* scenario (SKAB cavitation). The genuinely brief-blind *control* is "ctrl SKAB", unmarked. One-word fix in the caption; as printed it sends the reader to the wrong bar.
4. **Caption-vs-image imprecision, Fig. 3:** caption says "Blue chips are agent stages, grey chips deterministic scripts", but the drawing shows Step 0/1 ("main agent + scripts") grey and Step 3 ("data-processor + scripts") blue — two hybrid stages that the binary encoding misclassifies either way. Recolor the hybrids or reword ("grey chips mark the script-dominant ingestion and finalization steps").
5. **Mixed British/American spelling.** The paper is otherwise American ("behavior", "artifact" throughout, no "artefact"), but retains: *quantised/quantisation* (≈8×), *synchronised*, *itemised*, *synthesised*, *anonymised*, *defence* — and Fig. 7's evidence cell says "localised" while the body says "localizes". Harmonize to American English (AEI accepts either; not both).
6. **Em-dash/parenthetical density in the body.** 231 em-dash instances and ≈600 parentheticals in 57 pages of text; many paragraphs carry two to three dash-delimited insertions per sentence (Sections 7.2, 9.2, 9.6 are the worst). The non-sequitur parentheticals (e.g., the 9-line "An on-disk second batch…" insertion in Section 8.5) should become footnotes or released-README pointers.
7. **Float drift.** All floats stay within their sections (placeins works), but four drift 3 pages past first citation: Table 6 (cited p. 23, placed p. 26), Fig. 4 (cited p. 24, placed p. 27), Table 7 (cited p. 25, placed p. 29), Fig. 6 (cited p. 28, placed p. 31). Acceptable for print, but [!t]+`\clearpage`-free tuning or moving the load commands one paragraph earlier would tighten it. Table 9 is forward-referenced from Section 5 (p. 18) but appears on p. 46 — a 28-page gap; consider citing it only from Section 9 or moving it there.
8. **Half-empty pages:** p. 41 ends at ≈55% height (Fig. 10 deferred to p. 42); p. 58 ends at ≈55% (Table A2 on its own page). After M3 the appendix tables should pack onto one page.
9. **Mid-word hyphenation inside scaled table cells:** Table A2 hyphenates "throt-tling" and "col-lapse" in justified `p{}` columns; Table 6 has "construc-tion"/"deter-mined" wraps. Use `\raggedright` (as Table 2 correctly does) in A1/A2's narrow columns.
10. **Fig. 4 caption vs legend wording:** caption says "grey = controls", the in-figure legend says "controls (shaded rows)". Harmless, but align the two.
11. **Fig. 6 minor text nits:** in-panel annotation font ("Quantisation: 0.327 bar… (text only)") is ≈6 pt — at the floor; and the legend string "Volume Flow RateRMS" is missing a space ("…Flow Rate RMS").

**Not issues (checked and clean):** build log has zero warnings/overfulls; no undefined or multiply-defined references; figure/table/algorithm numbering strictly sequential; "Fig." vs "Figure" usage follows the start-of-sentence convention consistently; DETERMINED/COMPETING_SET/NEEDS_DATA small-caps usage is uniform; "artifact" spelling is uniform; in-figure "Khan et al., 2025" matches the bibliography year; highlights file has 5 bullets all ≤85 chars; all declarations (CRediT, competing interests, funding, data availability, AI-use) present; corresponding author and ead footnote correct.

## Verdict: MINOR REVISION

**Justification.** The presentation layer is fundamentally sound and unusually honest: figures are script-generated in one consistent Okabe–Ito palette, every single caption-vs-image number I checked (≈60 of them) matches the tables and text exactly, the build is warning-free, float discipline holds within sections, and the AEI back-matter is complete. What remains is fixable in days without touching results: (1) rewrite the abstract for self-containment, (2) lift Tables A1/A2 (and the borderline T9) off the resizebox floor by cutting their captions down to captions, (3) correct two caption inaccuracies (Fig. 10 asterisk; Fig. 3 chip colors), (4) harmonize the ≈13 stray British spellings, (5) un-split the keyword block and trim to six keywords, (6) relieve the dash/parenthetical density where it is worst. No figure needs redrawing from scratch; no claim is presented illegibly in the main text.
