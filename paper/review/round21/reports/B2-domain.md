## Paper Summary

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage agentic diagnosis pipeline for industrial processes: deterministic statistics (anomaly profiles, lag-compensated CCF, batch integrity), LLM competing-hypotheses reasoning with ≥3 hypotheses and quantitative elimination, evidence grading L1–L7 with weakest-link confidence binding, a four-condition anti-spurious-correlation filter, a three-state verdict protocol (DETERMINED / capped COMPETING_SET / NEEDS_DATA with named discriminating measurement), bounded repair loops, and physical verification. Around it the paper builds a machine-checked evaluation contract (truth isolation with a leakage sentinel, SHA-256 fingerprinting, stage-authored provenance-labeled artifacts, a 14-artifact completeness gate, and an artifact-consistency gate) and evaluates on 12 scenarios over SKAB, TEP, and IndPenSim (9 faults + 3 controls).

Headline results, deliberately bounded: Top-1 4/9 (provisional upper bound; identity-strict 3/9; joint worst-case floor 0/9 given run-directory identifier leakage), top-k 9/9 family-level (8/9 branch-localized), 3/3 controls pass (one blind), five capped verdicts each naming the discriminating measurement. A same-model ablation shows a bare LLM call over the pipeline's own blind statistical digest keyword-scores 9/9 vs the pipeline's 4/9 resolved — the paper's central concession — so the claimed contribution is the auditable, capped-honest process, not accuracy. Baseline provenance is presented as: classical PCA arm (deterministic script, byte-reproducible), FaultExplainer-protocol replication on the same GLM deployment, and single-call arms disclosed as "recorded replays of live runs"; the deterministic suite reproduces 45 recorded outputs byte-identically. One seeded random-draw re-execution (TEP IDV11) reproduced verdict state, actuator, and mechanism class within Δconfidence 0.02.

## Focus-Area Assessment

### (2) Novelty of the three-state evidence-graded protocol and the machine-checked evaluation contract — ADEQUATE AS SCOPED
The protocol's components (reject option, ACH, automation/trust levels, evidence hierarchies, consistency-based elimination) are pre-1980–2010 intellectual property and the paper says so explicitly, claiming only "a domain-specific, machine-checked instantiation … not their invention." That framing survives scrutiny. What is genuinely new, to my knowledge, in LLM-based industrial FDD: (i) cap-arithmetic binding of reported confidence to the weakest evidence level, with the agent-side vs machine-side cap-set gap (0.91/0.93 exceedances) reported as a measured finding about the authors' own two cap artifacts; (ii) the three-state verdict with a named-discriminating-measurement exit, quantified as an operating point on a risk–coverage curve, including the internally consistent forced-commitment counterfactual (6/9 with three specific wrong commitments; random-commitment expectation ≈6.08/9 — arithmetic checks out); (iii) the C1–C5 evaluation contract, in particular the completeness gate that makes the (disclosed and removed) artifact-fabrication shortcut permanently unreplayable. Verified contemporaneous competitors (FE, AgentRCA) have neither calibrated confidence nor cap semantics, so the combination claim in Table 1 holds. Under the honest ablation framing (bare call 9/9 vs pipeline 4/9 conceded in the abstract), the novelty claim is fair; the residual weakness — the process qualities being architectural properties, not independently validated outcomes (blinded expert adjudication outstanding) — is disclosed at every point of use.

### (3) Baseline provenance presentation — HONEST, ALMOST SUFFICIENT
The manuscript discloses: recorded replays (not live) for the LLM arms, same GLM deployment as the pipeline, single recorded executions, TEP-control coverage gap in the bare arm, one verbatim answer in Chinese, deterministic PCA (pure-Jacobi script + archived prototype agreement), and the 45/45 byte-identical double run correctly scoped to the deterministic arms only. This is more honest than is customary. What is missing from the manuscript: the numerical validation of the FE-protocol replication against FaultExplainer's **own committed outputs** (per the released verification gate: 21/21 checks, T² relative error ~1e-12, anomaly flags 500/500, contribution ~1e-13, and the discovery of FE's per-component clip-then-sum convention). That gate is precisely what licenses the label "replication of FaultExplainer's protocol"; without it in the text, the label rests on self-attestation. One sentence in §7.3 (plus pointers in Table 5's caption and the data-availability statement) fixes this. The arms are honestly named ("FE-protocol replication (GLM)", never attributed to FE's GPT-4o/o1-preview numbers), and the paper never claims superiority over FE — it even prints the FE-official-prompt arm above IDD's TEP Top-1 (5/6 vs 3/6) and reads it descriptively.

### (4) Domain soundness of per-mechanism signatures and the IDV4 case study — SOUND
- **IDV4**: the recorded chain (XMV_10 sustained +6.8σ step with reactor-temperature spike z=+10.5, loop-externals flat) matches the documented IDV4 mechanism; the energy-balance necessity argument (observed coolant-flow rise with conserved reactor temperature ⇒ inlet-temperature step or UA loss) is algebraically correct (Q = UA·LMTD = ṁcpΔT), and the UA-fork quantification (approach <1.5 K ⇒ coolant inlet ≳93 °C vs the ≈26 K approach observed — internally consistent with a ~120 °C reactor and XMEAS_21-type coolant outlet near ~95 °C) is a legitimate quantitative discriminator, correctly held below the elimination threshold because a UA loss can redistribute LMTD. Capping at 0.63 rather than forcing is the protocol working as designed, and the T² 48.8%/SPE 100% replication matches the classical "weak T², strong Q" record for IDV4.
- **Within-family form discrimination (IDV4/11/14)**: variance-burst-with-frozen-means (IDV11) and quantised-stepping limit cycle (IDV14; VR ×189/116/222, lag tightening 1→5, 633/800 quantised steps) are the textbook stiction fingerprint per the cited Horch/Hägglund/Choudhury literature; separating three dynamic forms on one physical channel is the paper's best anti-recall evidence and the argument (label recall cannot produce three distinct statistical pictures) is sound, with the residual cause-table-recall channel disclosed.
- **IDV3**: all means ≤0.92σ with variance concentration in the stripper steam loop is consistent with IDV3's documented weak signature; the survivor containing the D/E-feed-temperature truth family inside a stripper-feed-branch hypothesis, and the branch-localized 8/9 discount, are correctly and conservatively reported. (The text's "reactor-feed D stream" is topologically correct — stream 2 is a reactor fresh feed — though a stream-number gloss would help readers.)
- **SKAB cases**: the throttling case-study figure (flow plateau −3.13% at quantisation floor, directional-only current co-drop −0.115 A p=0.10, 0.327-bar pressure quantisation masking head direction) supports the two-survivor cap; the cavitation reading (intermittent collapse, pump load held, +0.23 K consistent with local dissipation, named missing suction-pressure/valve-position channels) is physically coherent and correctly grounded in the AE-monitoring literature.
- **IndPenSim batch 93**: the paper's handling is exemplary — the dataset's truth channel is mechanism-free, the actuator-side case definition is disclosed as possibly under-describing the batch, the pipeline's cooling-loop verdict is identity-discounted (3/9 strict), and the divergent bare-LLM/PCA answers (acid-feed pH loop; heating-water flow heading SPE contributions) are printed rather than buried.

### (5) Digest-priming and shared-instrument caveats — ADEQUATE
Both caveats are present where they matter: §6.4 states the bare arm "measures reading a good digest, not raw-data ability" with the raw-data-only arm pre-registered; §7.3 states the structural caveat that both arms are scored by the same author-designed keyword instrument, so the 9/9-vs-4/9 contrast is instrument-relative; Table 4's caption warns that "strict" for the baseline arms counts any keyword hit whereas the Top-1 row counts resolved verdicts only; and the instrument's circularity (keywords calibrated on v1-era runs of the same scenarios) is quantified via leave-one-token-out (Top-1 bounded 3/9–4/9). The forced-commitment counterfactual further decomposes the cap cost from the digest quality. Two sharpenings requested below (M3, m3), but the disclosure structure is adequate.

**M2. The FE-replication's numerical validation is absent from the manuscript (provenance presentation gap).**
The strongest authenticity evidence for the "FE-protocol replication (GLM)" arm — that its feature-extraction path was verified check-by-check against FaultExplainer's own committed outputs (21/21; T² rel. err ~1e-12; contribution ~1e-13; identical trigger indices; FE's clip-then-sum convention discovered) — exists in the released verification layer but is nowhere stated in the paper. **M3. Filter-attribution slightly overreaches its tested evidence.**
§4.5 states the anti-spurious filter "is the reason control-loop correlations are not reported as root causes" in the benchmark, yet the bare-LLM arm — which runs no filter — also produced no false alarms on the two covered controls (disclosed two pages later). The filter's necessity is not isolated by the corpus (the filter-disabled arm is pre-registered, not run). Keep the design claim but bind its wording, e.g., "the filter is the mechanism by which the pipeline bars these correlations from causal citation," and cross-reference the bare arm's control passes and the pre-registered arm where the stronger sentence appears.
