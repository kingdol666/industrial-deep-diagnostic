## 8. Assessment (AEI empirical-methods taste)

Strengths: uncertainty quantification is correct and uniform (one z-constant everywhere,
values reproduce to the decimal); the pre-revision qualifier appears on every headline
surface; the C1 chronology is itemized to the journal timestamp and survives independent
recomputation; the two-layer cap description — including the unflattering detail that the
agent never documented a DET cap and that the gate's flat 0.90 silently covers ND — is the
kind of disclosure most system papers omit; §7–§8 lead with the result that hurts
(bare-LLM 9/9 > pipeline 6/9) and bound every claim (single executions, repair selection
effect, author-designed instruments, no significance tests across differing sets).

Residual limitations are inherent to the design (n=9 with wide intervals; one model family;
keyword substring scoring) and are disclosed rather than obscured; none rises to a blocking
defect. Remaining items are presentational.
