## 4. Float discipline and page packing

- All 8 figures and 7 of 9 tables use top preference (`[!t]`); appendix tables use `[p]`; float pages are top-aligned (`\@fptop=0pt`); `placeins [section]` confines floats to sections. Rendered placements are top-of-page everywhere. No `[h]`/`[b]` floats, no bottom floats.
- **Stub page p42 (the known one)**: single widow line ("failure modes that produce false alarms in naive correlation-driven diagnosis.") atop an otherwise empty page, caused by the Section-7 `placeins` barrier flushing the near-page-size Table 7 onto p43. **Judgment: acceptable preprint typography** — this is the `preprint,review` build, Elsevier re-typesets for production, and the cause is structural (a 9-row table with a long caption cannot share a page). Not blocking.
- **Stub page p55 (not previously flagged)**: 5 lines (~90% empty) ending Section 8, same mechanism (Section-8 barrier + Fig 8's deferred float page on p56). Same class, same verdict, but the paper now contains **two** near-empty pages plus two half-empty appendix float pages — worth one repacking pass.
- Appendix pages p72 (Table A1) and p73 (Table A2) each hold one table and ~50% whitespace; the source comment "% Float repack: [p] lets A1 and A2 share a single appendix float page" did not materialise (A1+A2 exceed one page, so the comment is stale). Cosmetic.
- Citation→placement drift is bounded by `placeins`: worst case Fig 8, cited p51, placed p56 (still inside Section 8); Fig 6/7 cited p44, placed p46/47. The preamble's float comment discloses this preprint-build behaviour. Acceptable.
