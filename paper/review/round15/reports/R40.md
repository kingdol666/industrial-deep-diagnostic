# R40 — Sceptical General Review (Round 15, blind)

Paper: "Trustworthy agentic diagnosis for industrial processes: evidence-graded competing-hypothesis
reasoning evaluated against per-fault literature baselines" (main.tex, 75 pp compiled).
Reviewer role: AEI, fresh/blind, whole-paper read; instruction: verify description vs what the
system actually does. I read the full TeX source, the rendered pages (abstract, C1 chronology,
all result figures, architecture figures), and performed ~18 claim-vs-reality checks against the
repository. I read nothing under paper/review/ except this briefing.

## Overall assessment

This is an unusually verifiable systems paper. Every one of my numeric, artifact-path, chronology
and architecture spot checks matched the released repository exactly — including several claims
that a dishonest paper would simply have rounded in its own favour (pre-revision keyword Top-1
5/9 vs calibrated 6/9; bare-LLM 9/9 beating the pipeline's 6/9; 2-of-3 control coverage in the
bare-LLM arm; the outstanding seeded-retest execution contract; the single verdict-type
compliance miss; the released internal-audit document that originally found brief leakage and a
degenerate judge gate). The prominent negatives promised by the abstract are actually delivered
in the body. The weaknesses that remain are presentation-level (abstract precision on the one
mechanism-level miss; density of the C1 chronology paragraph) and epistemic-level (author-designed
rubric/keywords, single model family, single execution per scenario, no independent expert
anchoring) — the latter are disclosed plainly and bounded, but they cap the paper at minor
revision rather than acceptance-as-is from my seat.

Novelty: moderate and honestly framed. The competing-hypotheses discipline (Heuer), anti-spurious
conditions (Hill), abstention (Chow) and automation-trust framing (Parasuraman; Lee & See) are
conceded as ancestry in §2.4; the three-state protocol with gate-enforced ceilings and the
machine-checked evaluation contract (C1–C5) are the genuinely new engineering contribution, and
§2.2's positioning against FaultExplainer (strict single verdict vs top-3-with-alias; scoring the
PCA-excluded IDV3/IDV4) and AgentRCA (calibrated confidence + external truth-comparison harness
absent there) is fair and specific. The abstract's concession that a bare same-model call scores
9/9 — above the pipeline — is the paper's most credibility-building statement; AEI reviewers
should reward, not punish, this.

## Verdict rationale

Everything checkable is true. What keeps this at minor revision rather than publish-as-is:
(a) the abstract's only structural imprecision (the IDV3 miss is left to arithmetic); (b) the C1
chronology paragraph — the paper's most contestable integrity passage — is accurate but written
as a ~450-word dual-timezone sentence chain that reviewers will not thank the author for;
(c) inherent limits (author-designed instruments, one model, n=9, single execution) are disclosed
but necessarily bound the strength of the contribution. All are text-level fixes.

## MUST-FIX (blocking, ≤8)

1. Abstract (p.1–2): the IDV3 exclusion is never stated — "ranks the true mechanism among the
   candidates in two more; the three scenarios whose data cannot discriminate…" leaves the one
   mechanism-level miss to be inferred by arithmetic. Evidence: verified topk=8/9 with tep_d03
   topk=False in metrics.json/keyword_sensitivity.json. Add one clause ("in the third the true
   mechanism is not among the survivors") — the body already reports it honestly.
2. §5 (C1, pp. 25–26): the chronology items (i)–(iv) are timestamp-accurate (verified against
   journal.jsonl, git 221d061, and the gradings' run_dirs) but are a single dense paragraph of
   nested dual-timezone parentheticals; convert to a compact timeline table (event / UTC /
   UTC+8 / evidence pointer). An unreadable integrity paragraph reads as evasion even when it
   is not.

## SHOULD-FIX (≤6)

1. §6.2: disclose that R4's verdict-type check scores controls via the grader's control_pass
   sentinel (expected set ["NORMAL"] is semantic) — currently only a code comment in
   judge-rubric.mjs.
2. Fig 6 / Fig 8: the vermillion FE-wrong cell and the amber capped-CS cell are close in hue and
   in grayscale; add a hatch or letter code.
3. §3.1 and §7.5 paragraphs run 300–450 words with multi-clause lists; split at the scoping /

4. §8 film-line anecdote: label it explicitly "author-attested, unreleased" to separate it from
