# R6 — Industrial-Informatics Systems Review (Round 7, blind)

Reviewer mandate: architecture/module-contract clarity and **system fidelity** — every statement
about the system must be true of the real codebase. I read the full paper (`paper/main.tex`,
70 pp) and the rendered pages for my focus areas (p-06, p-07, p-11, p-12, p-14, p-15, p-16,
p-20), then verified claims against the repository. I read nothing else under `paper/review/`.

---

## 1. Overall assessment

This is, by a wide margin, the most code-faithful systems paper I have reviewed in this cycle.
The architecture chapter (§3) makes the layer map, each module's individual responsibility, and
the inter-module contracts unmistakably clear: five planes (clients → engines → orchestrator →
pipeline → audit), a module-contracts table (Tab. 2) with Consumes/Produces/Gate filenames, and a
checkpoint table (Tab. 3) whose predicates are mechanical. I verified the tables against the
orchestrator skill and the released run directories, and they match line-for-line. The dispatch
wording is careful and honest: 18 skill packages / 14 agents, of which the diagnostic path
dispatches eight specialist sub-agents with the VLM inside the data-processor's Phase 5.5 — all
three numbers are true of the repository.

The few fidelity problems I found are one-line wording issues, but one of them is a literal
claim about released artifacts that a reader can machine-falsify, so I cannot call it
publish-as-is.

## 3. Findings

### MUST-FIX (blocking)

1. **p. 11 (§3.2) and the prompt-knowledge paragraph of the Discussion: "no L6 evidence item
   appears in any released evidence file" is falsified by one released artifact.** The canonical,
   graded IDV14 run (`workspace/diagnostic-runs/202609150454479_bench_tep_d14_reactor_valve_sticking/04_diagnostics/evidence.json`)
   contains exactly one rank-6 evidence item sourced “TEP基准过程知识(L5/L6)” ("reactor-cooling-valve
   stiction is a known TEP fault mode"), explicitly marked corroboration-only. The operative
   claim — *no verdict rests on external evidence* — survives (the item is not a criterion), but
   the literal sentence is machine-checkable and false as written, and it is stated twice.
   Reword, e.g. "no L6-designated item enters any surviving chain as decision evidence" (and keep
   the corroboration-only item, ideally footnoting it — it is actually a nice honesty exhibit).

### SHOULD-FIX (non-blocking)

1. **p. 11 (§3.2), same paragraph:** "the TEP-era runs record explicit probe-failure or
   empty-retrieval degradations in their event logs" — true of the TEP runs whose ontology stage
   engaged the channel (d00-0914, d01-0915, d14-0915), but six of the seven canonical 0914 TEP
   runs used ontology fast-reuse (event `clarification_auto_inferred`) and are silent about RAG.
   Scope the sentence ("runs whose ontology stage engaged the channel record…"), otherwise an
   auditor re-running the corpus will find silent logs and read overclaim.
2. **Tab. 2 (p. 15), "Visual evidence (3.5)" row:** listing `vlm-visual-analyzer` as its own row
   invites counting nine dispatched sub-agents, exactly the misreading §3.1 warns against. Add
   "(inside data-processor Phase 5.5)" to the row.
3. **Tab. 2 (p. 15), Ontology row:** "(+ clarification_needed.json)" carries no path while the
   caption promises run-directory-relative paths; in every released run it is
   `00_input/clarification_needed.json`.
4. **Fig. 2 (p. 14):** the bottom row reads right-to-left (Step 9 leftmost). The caption
   explains it, but at print size the leftward arrows initially read as backwards flow; a
   left-to-right second row (or heavier arrowheads) would remove the hiccup. Cosmetic.
5. **§3.1 vs Tab. 2 numbering:** the text says the diagnostic path is "Steps 2–8.5" while Tab. 2
   also lists Step 9 (finalise) — fine, but one clause noting that Step 9 is the deterministic
   finaliser rather than a dispatched agent (it is in Tab. 2's agent column already) would make
   the eight-sub-agent count airtight for skimmers.

### Positive observations worth keeping

- Tab. 3 is the paper's best systems artifact: every predicate is re-runnable by a reader, and
  all nine match the orchestrator skill exactly.
- The scoping clarifications in §3.1 (18/14/8, VLM inside Phase 5.5, skill ≠ prompt) preempt the
  three most common misreadings of skill-agent systems; all three are true of the repo.
- The RAG honesty (degradations recorded, SKAB retrieval successful yet unused) is verifiably
  true in the event logs and artifacts — a model of honest negative reporting, once MUST-FIX 1's
  sentence is tightened.
- Figures 1–2 are legible at 110 dpi, use a consistent Okabe-Ito palette, and every number in
  them (14 engines, 18/14, 400/409, best-of-3/global-5/0.50, ENDORSED/PASS gates) checks out
  against code.

## 4. Verdict

MINOR_REVISION. The architecture and contract chapters are faithful to the codebase to an
unusual degree — 9/9 checkpoint rows, 14/14 engine ids, both typed error codes, all repair-governance
numbers, the grader's 14-artifact set, and the model-assignment claim verify exactly. One
literally false sentence about L6 evidence items in released files (stated twice) plus four
small clarity/scoping fixes separate this from publish-as-is.
