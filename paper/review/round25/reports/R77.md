  One qualifying clause would close the only asymmetry the keyword-sensitivity disclosure leaves

  §6.2 (correctly) states and the grader implements; align the two sentences.
- S5 (§5 Execution form, p.~24): the 8/3 field/note split depends on classifying tep_d04/tep_d07
