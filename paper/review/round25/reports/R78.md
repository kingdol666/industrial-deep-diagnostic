Paper: `paper/main.tex` (75 rendered pages). Focus assigned: §2–§3 architecture claims vs codebase
and canonical graded runs; §5 execution-form disclosure; package census; RAG two-mechanism split;
CP-1..CP-9; repair governance; model assignment. Blind: I read only the briefing, the tex source,
the rendered page PNGs, and the repository/ground truth. I read nothing else under `paper/review/`.

## 1. Overall assessment

This is an unusually verifiable systems paper: nearly every architectural and execution-form claim I
checked resolves to a concrete artifact in the repository, and the disclosed negatives (inline
execution, serialized 5a/5b, per-run RAG degradation, one repair, one missing marker log) are all
real. I performed **16 claim-vs-reality spot checks** (Section 4); **all load-bearing claims
matched**. Two wording-level imprecisions (Section 5, should-fix) are the only defects found in my
scope. No claim in my focus areas was false.

## 7. Should-fix (non-blocking)

- **S1 (§3.2, p.11–12, RAG paragraph).** The sentence contrasts the four TEP fast-path runs ("no
  fallback event in their logs") with the two SKAB runs that "lack the RAG artifact via the same
  fast path" — but the four TEP fast-path runs also lack `rag_deep_understanding.json` (verified).
  Every stated fact is true; one clause ("all six runs lack the artifact; none record events") would
  prevent a reader from inferring the TEP fast-path runs have one.
- **S2 (§5 Execution form, p.28).** The "dedicated field on eight runs, disclosure or remediation
  notes on three" split depends on classifying tep_d01's final-event `disclosure` key as a field
  rather than a note; the marker keys also vary (`harness`, `mode`, `harness_mode`,
  `execution_mode`, `inline_execution`). The load-bearing counts (11/12; cavitation the only gap)
  are exact, but a one-line footnote enumerating the per-run keys would make the census
  reader-auditable.
- **S3 (§6.4, p.33, implementation).** "A complete 12-scenario sweep takes … 56–220 minutes …
  (median ≈109 across the twelve canonical runs; event-log spans)" — precisely correct for the 12
  canonical runs, but the corpus also holds earlier non-canonical v1 runs with much longer spans;
  "canonical" is stated, a pointer to the superseded runs would pre-empt a reviewer recomputing
  over all 40 directories.
- **S4 (Table 4, "Canonical gradings" row).** "from 09-14 21:17Z" is the first *later* batch for the
  09-14 runs; the three 09-15 executions were graded at 09-15 06:22Z (also after the switch). The
  narrative "every canonical grading after the switch" is correct; adding the 06:22Z batch would
  make the row complete.

## 8. Verdict

From an industrial-informatics systems standpoint this paper is in exemplary shape: the module map
(18 packages / 14 agents / 8-dispatched diagnostic path), the CP table, the repair counters, the
engine-layer contract, and — rarest — the execution-form confession (inline stages, serialized
5a/5b, per-run RAG degradation, keyword-calibration chronology) all reproduce from the repository
without a single false statement found. The four should-fix items are one-clause wording tightenings.
