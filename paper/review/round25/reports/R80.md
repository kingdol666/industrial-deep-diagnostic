# R80 — Skeptical General Review (Round 25, fresh/blind)

Reviewer role: whole-paper read — abstract promise vs delivery, coherence, novelty, writing,
reproducibility/ethics — and, above all, whether the paper's description matches what the
system actually does. Verified against the released repository tree; read only the paper,
the page PNGs, and the ground-truth files listed in the briefing. Nothing under
`paper/review/` other than the briefing and page images.

---

## 1. Summary of the paper

The paper presents IDD, a nine-stage multi-agent diagnosis pipeline for industrial processes
(ontology → deterministic statistics → competing-hypothesis reasoning → dual audit → report →
HTML), with L1–L7 evidence grading, a four-condition anti-spurious filter, bounded repair
governance, and a machine-checked 12-scenario benchmark (SKAB 2+1, TEP 6+1, IndPenSim 1+1) with
per-fault FaultExplainer baselines and three model-controlled baseline arms. Headline: 6/9
Top-1 (5/9 pre-revision keywords), top-k 8/9, 3/3 controls, rubric 97.9, judge 93.1, IDV3
reported as a mechanism-level miss, and a same-model bare-LLM ablation that scores 9/9 — i.e.
the pipeline's claimed value is explicitly *not* accuracy but auditability, calibrated
uncertainty, and gated process.

## 2. Abstract

- **Length**: 246 words by my count (limit 250). OK.
- **Promise vs delivery**: every abstract claim I traced is delivered somewhere in the paper
  AND in the released tree (see spot checks). The IDV3 miss is stated in the abstract as "a
  mechanism-level miss"; 5/9 and 4/6 pre-revision numbers are carried unspun in the abstract,
  contributions, results, comparison, discussion, and conclusion (7 occurrences by my count).
- The single strongest sentence — "Internal consistency is machine-verified against the
  released execution proofs" — is accurate but only in the internal-consistency sense that
  §5(C5) then states with exemplary clarity ("deliberately not an independent re-execution of
  the reasoning layer"). A casual abstract reader could over-read it. Minor phrasing hardening
  recommended (e.g. "internally machine-verified").
- The most damaging negative (bare same-model call 9/9 > pipeline 6/9) is in the abstract
  itself. This is the opposite of overclaiming.

## 4. Novelty

- vs **FaultExplainer**: the per-fault comparison is fair-minded — it transcribes FE's regimes
  (7/11, 9/11 with candidates+alias; 8/11 no-candidate lenient, with FE's own 10/13

  differences everywhere Register A is invoked, and reads the table "descriptively, not as a
  superiority claim". The FE-official-code replication on the same model (5/6, different miss

- vs **multi-agent LLM RCA** (AgentRCA, Gong et al.): positioning is current (July 2026
  contemporaneous work), the table's (iii)+(iv) differentiators are real (calibrated
  three-state verdicts; machine-verifiable evaluation contract), and the text concedes
  AgentRCA names uncertainty as future work rather than overstating.
- §2.4 (Parasuraman, Lee&See, Heuer, Hill, Chow, OntoCAPE) honestly frames IDD as "a
  domain-specific, machine-checked instantiation … not their invention". This section
  strengthens trust; the self-scoring Table 1 (IDD all-yes vs every other row) is defensible
  cell-by-cell but reads self-congratulatory; a footnote softening "Repro. gate = partial" for
  classical FDD would help (taste, not fact).

## 8. Remaining concerns (all minor)

1. **Fig 8 rounding inconsistency** (48.75→"48" vs 4.75→"5"). Cosmetic, but it is the kind of
   detail this paper otherwise gets right.
2. **Table 2 first row provenance**: "Calibrated set applied" is an attestation, not a
   journal/git record; caption should say so (one clause).
3. **Abstract's "machine-verified"** — add "internally" or equivalent (§5 C5 already states
   the scope precisely).
4. **Table 1 self-scoring** (IDD all-yes) — consider a caveat footnote; the text already
   argues each cell, so this is taste only.
5. **Single-execution, single-model evidence base** — the paper discloses this everywhere and
   bounds accuracy via the ablation; as a skeptical reader I would still have liked ≥1
   current-era repeat executed rather than pre-registered (the INSUFFICIENT-RUNS gap is
   disclosed, which mitigates). Not blocking given the honesty of the framing.
6. **Author-designed instruments** (rubric, keywords, allowed verdict sets) — disclosed as
   such; independent expert anchoring remains outstanding and is labelled as the next step.

## 9. Verdict

I came to this paper instructed to be skeptical, and specifically to check whether its
description matches what the system does. Across 13 spot checks spanning numbers, artifact
paths, chronology, and architecture-vs-code, I found zero mismatches — including several
claims (11/12 inline markers with the exact exception named; 21 chunks vs rag_available=false;
repair events only in the one control; 109-minute median recomputed from raw event logs) that
only an author confident in the released tree would state so precisely. The paper's
load-bearing negatives (bare-LLM superiority on accuracy, IDV3 miss, single compliance miss,
keyword-calibration history, removed fabrication shortcut, internal-consistency-only
verification scope) are disclosed prominently and verified. The residual issues are one
figure-rounding fix, two one-clause provenance/phrasing fixes, and taste-level items.

**Recommendation: MINOR_REVISION (favorable).** I am willing to sign a favorable report once
the minor items above are addressed; none touches the validity of the reported results.
